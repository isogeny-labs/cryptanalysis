BUDGET TABLE  C_dec(m,p) = max per-decomposition oracle cost to beat negation-rho
Entries are log2(C_dec_max)  (i.e. the oracle may cost up to 2^entry ops).
A NEGATIVE entry => IC cannot beat rho at that (m,p) for ANY oracle (budget < 1 op).
'LA' column: does the linear-algebra phase alone stay sub-rho? (needs a<1/4)
