## Recorded finding and its scope

The quartic and octic j=1728 factor discriminants have an exact place-by-place decomposition above the unramified common rational primes 7, 11, 23, and 31. In particular, **23 contributes to both integer norms but to no common prime ideal** of the degree-18 source field. The rational-prime gcd from [the root-support certificate](../../experiments/hecke-432-j1728-root-prime-support-20261026/RESULT.md) therefore overstates local overlap at 23.
