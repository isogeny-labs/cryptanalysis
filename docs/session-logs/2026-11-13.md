### Decisions made (and why)
- The headers keep their size and layout, and the version is carried by the magic plus `relation_size` together. One fread reads both versions, and neither field alone can make a reader accept the other format.
- An old journal is migrated on open, not appended in v1. Writers emit only v2 and a single file never mixes formats. The temp file, fsync, rename and parent fsync keep a crash from leaving a half-migrated journal.
- Binary v1 fixtures were not committed (AGENTS.md, data files). The tests write v1 from the spec, and the base-binary fixtures are recorded by sha256 in the note.
- Entries are reserved at 8 per relation: the measured mean is 6.2 to 7.4 and the maximum 11, where §3.5 estimated about 16.
