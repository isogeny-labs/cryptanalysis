# Prospective mechanism amendment

Date: 2026-11-18

Status: frozen before any natural row. This amendment does not relax any gate in
`PROTOCOL.md`; it adds six separately paired mechanism panels suggested by the
archived Xedni evidence. A mechanism panel can identify a lead, but only the
primary rank/dependence/recovery endpoints and decision rule in `PROTOCOL.md`
can classify the finite-range decay model as defeated. [pcc:unverified]

## Shared denominator and anti-selection rules

All arms in a panel share the source curve over `F_p`, prime-order subgroup,
target, ordered point tuple, random prefix, candidate-bank size `K`, and rank
budgets. `K` is fixed over `p`. Every proposed candidate, rejection, duplicate,
public group operation, rank call, timeout, and byte of retained state is
charged. Report both conditional-on-admission rates and unconditional rates per
proposed source tuple. [pcc:unverified]

Selectors may use only named public coordinates and invariants and are fixed
before rank, dependence, or recovery outcomes. Deduplicate Q-isomorphic models
and duplicate fixed-coordinate relation vectors without removing their
construction cost. Each selected arm is paired with a uniform random member of
the identical candidate bank and with a permuted-score null. Direct finite-field
relation search precedes the lift and is a mandatory costed baseline because
every admitted rational relation reduces to a public source relation.
[pcc:unverified]

## M1: normalized-discriminant and height bank

For a constant-size public bank, compare:

- balanced/canonical lift;
- uniform bank member;
- minimum construction-equation discriminant;
- minimum global-minimal discriminant;
- minimum `log(abs(Delta_min))/max_i hhat(P_i)`.

Retain the construction discriminant and global-minimal discriminant separately.
Canonical heights must be computed on an explicitly certified isomorphism to
the minimal model. Record the height Gram matrix, regulator, conductor, and any
minimum-height lower-bound status. This is the only panel aimed directly at the
height-ratio premise used by JKSST Lemma 4.1. A decreasing ratio is only a
mechanism signal; useful target-bearing recovery must still replicate.
[pcc:unverified]

## M2: quotient-compatible local conditions

Extend the primary `A_none`, `B_reverse_mestre`, and `P_neutral_crt` comparison
with:

- `I_incompatible`, deliberately mismatching a shared torsion quotient as a
  known-negative control;
- strict reverse-Mestre and the paper's `+1` and `+2` variants;
- a pairwise-coprime local-order canary, which makes the common-quotient check
  vacuous without pretending it was tested.

For every auxiliary prime retain the exact curve order, good-reduction check,
incidence rank, common quotient orders and images, construction attempts, and
rejections. Match `B`, `P`, and `I` on the same auxiliary primes and CRT modulus.
An incompatible row cannot enter the primary estimand. [pcc:unverified]

## M3: public nonlinear coordinate selector

Freeze a coefficient-independent score over a constant family of integral
coordinate shifts or nonlinear lift charts. Compare the score winner, a random
family member, a permuted-score winner, and the balanced lift. The score cannot
use a relation vector, rank output, p-adic defect computed from an already found
relation, or the target scalar. Record whether a directly testable source
relation was available before every rational hit. This panel is successful only
if it predicts held-out nontrivial useful relations after all selection work is
charged. [pcc:unverified]

## M4: `r=7..9` nonisomorphic plane cubics

Use a fixed-size genuinely nonisomorphic plane-cubic bank, paired by prefixes of
one ordered nine-point tuple. Compare a random member with the M1 selectors and
report per-candidate and any-of-`K` rates. No rank row is admissible until a
certified cubic-to-Jacobian construction maps every selected point, maps the
origin, checks good reduction, and replays source relations. A Jacobian rank
without the point map is not evidence. [pcc:unverified]

## M5: small-degree isogeny neighbors

For prospectively frozen small degrees `ell`, construct every tested neighbor
and explicit maps `phi` and dual `psi`. Admit a source subgroup of order `q`
only after checking `gcd(ell,q)=1`, `psi(phi(P))=[ell]P`, kernel intersection,
and target recovery. Compare the transported lift with the source curve, a
coordinate-isomorphic control, and a public-invariant-selected neighbor. Charge
neighbor enumeration and all maps. Without a smaller normalized
height/discriminant or a replicated recovery effect, this is only an equivalent
DLP representation. [pcc:unverified]

## M6: public predictor for nonisomorphic rank-one families

Train only on a separately named development panel and freeze a predictor that
uses pre-rank public invariants. On fresh holdout seeds compare its chosen model
with a random candidate, a permuted-label predictor, and direct source relation
testing. Retain every timeout and verify whether the directly testable public
relation appeared before the chosen rational relation. No rank-informed or
relation-informed selection is allowed in the holdout. Failure to beat direct
testing closes this mechanism even if it enriches rank-one curves.
[pcc:unverified]

## Per-panel disposition

Each panel ends in exactly one of `BLOCKED`, `NULL_IN_SCOPE`, `MECHANISM_ONLY`,
or `REPLICATED_USEFUL_SIGNAL`. None of those labels is
`DEFEATED_IN_SCOPE`. The latter remains reserved for the complete primary rule
in `PROTOCOL.md`, including the powered exponent bound, adversarial treatment of
unresolved ranks, fresh replication, two-tool certification, and total-cost
comparison. [pcc:unverified]
