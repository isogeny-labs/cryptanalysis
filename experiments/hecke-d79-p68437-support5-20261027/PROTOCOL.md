# p68437 support-five rank experiment

The frozen p68437 conductor-four class has 40 verified points, 20 degree-2/5 base rows, 200 signed direct relations and 196 degree-13 outside-trace cancellations at support at most four. Their combined rational and mod-109 rank is 34. This experiment tests whether adding all canonical signed support-five direct or outside-trace relations increases either rank. Retain the separate arm ranks, combined rank, exact candidate rows, trials, zero-yield cells, stage timings, and the six-dimensional prior kernel. A rank gain is an exact finite relation-space result, not a target-dependent solve.

The producer indexes every signed pair sum and queries it with every signed triple sum. Restrict to disjoint indices and require the triple to contain the smallest of the five indices, then normalize the first coefficient to `+1` and deduplicate. Verify every recovered candidate by the curve group law and its class-point row. This method should enumerate the same canonical patterns as an exhaustive `C(40,5)·2^4` scan, but the independent audit must check that equality explicitly with a different enumeration order using Sage points and pair-sum lookup. The audit also recomputes the new matrix ranks over `Q` and `F_109` and checks every row equation on the curve.

The parent class, source, analysis and audit receipts are SHA-256 pinned in `inputs.json` before any new enumeration. The input laws and source identities stay fixed. Use the repository checked Sage launcher for all Sage jobs, retain `./sage --runtime-info`, cap producer and audit commands at 1,800 seconds, and preserve any failure/timeout status. `candidate_id` and `target_online_ms` stay null.
