# Ascending degree-13 source-prefix experiment

The two cases p8597 and p12197 have base-plus-direct rational rank 39 in the frozen five-case panel. This experiment asks how many degree-13 Hecke source fibers must be lifted, in fixed ascending class-point index order, before signed outside-trace cancellations of support at most four raise the combined rational row rank to 40. This is an adaptive stopping policy defined before the new producer run; earlier full-census receipts informed the case choice and are not holdout data.

The checked Sage producer rebuilds the degree-13 functions, loads and hash-checks the frozen 40 class points and direct rows, then lifts one source fiber at a time. It checks factor pattern, all 14 lifted points, Hecke sum, modular polynomial, Frobenius orbit traces and the frozen full-census entry for that source. After adding a source, enumerate only signed combinations containing that newest source, with support 1..4, first coefficient +1 and remaining coefficients in [-1,+1] order. Verify each zero-trace candidate as a class-point relation. Recompute ranks over QQ and Fq, record source-lift/search/rank wall times and cumulative operations, then stop at first QQ rank 40 or after all 40 sources. Preserve zero-yield prefixes and failures.

Run through `./sage` with `gtimeout 1800`, saving `./sage --runtime-info` before measured work. The direct baseline is a separately measured, hash-pinned preparation input; report its prior costs separately. Compare source count and exact trial count to full-fiber enumeration, not a claimed complete IC or rho online speedup. The independent audit uses the hash-pinned parent as its fixed direct baseline and a candidate-set consistency reference, and recomputes every prefix candidate from the newly lifted trace sums using the separate pure-Python group law, then checks source ordering, first-hit status, row equations and ranks. Candidate ID and target online time are null.
