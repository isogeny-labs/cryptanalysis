# Trace-reduced W8 partial families have no four-term quadratic identity

The exact N41 census found **no nonzero Boolean quadratic annihilator with at most four monomial terms** in the witness-centered width-16 two-pair partial families or any of seven nonempty sampled region quadruples. Each family is represented by 821 monomials: one constant, 40 nontrace coordinate terms, and 780 squarefree products. The witness families contain 120 and 128 distinct reciprocal coordinates; the sampled families contain 96 or 128. An independently rebuilt Python audit agrees on the witness's two families and sample zero's two families. This closes the specific sparse-quadratic shortcut for these frozen regions and shifts the next search toward constraints that retain source labels and point signs.

The source is the archived diagonal W8 four-pair table on `EC1N41Ckb1h7d5e80d3c4d0`, over `GF(2)[t]/(t^41+t^3+1)`, with raw public preimage index 1 and reciprocal target `344545435656`. Four rotations use 30,952 signed pair states per slot; each tested region admits 16 state IDs per slot. The witness state IDs are `(17678,155,6169,16990)`. This source and public point are distinct from the fixed-seed seven-pair point used in the preceding functional-root SAT iteration. The nine region quadruples are the witness-centered region and eight deterministic samples, one of which has an empty partial family and is omitted from the signature census. Every nonempty sampled region had zero exact S3 coordinate hits; the witness region retained one.

The first frozen implementation counted the field's universally zero trace coordinate and its 40 products as one-term identities. Its output and the plain-Python import failure remain in the adjacent first-version directory. The version-two worker verifies `Tr(c)=0` and bit zero for each reciprocal, removes bit zero from its monomial basis, and leaves the region starts, partial cardinalities, and S3 hit counts unchanged. A one-worker monitored native run completed in **11.710 s**, with sampled combined RSS **45,580,288 bytes**, below its 120 s and 4 GiB caps; monitoring was intact. These are local diagnostic costs on an unisolated host. `python3 -B verify.py` checks the frozen source and binary hashes, both raw receipts, the version-one comparison, and the independent audit.

The census rules out only identities of Boolean degree at most two and monomial support at most four on these exact finite families. It does not test denser quadratic relations or larger source regions. Its practical implication is specific: adding very sparse quadratic equations from width-16 partial outputs is unlikely to give the early whole-family rejection needed by the N131 method.
