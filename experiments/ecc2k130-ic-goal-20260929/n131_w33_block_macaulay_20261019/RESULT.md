# N131 W33 block Macaulay transfer and resource gate

The exact two-left-bit block expansion transferred to the N131 W33 source: it produced **1,619 squarefree ANF terms of degree at most four** on each of the frozen constructed-positive and public resultant rows. With both left bits and all **32 right-code bits free**, degree-four and degree-five Macaulay reductions completed under one worker, 180 seconds, and 4 GiB, but found **no constant-one certificate on either row**. The known positive code pair `(3,16722)` was retained. An independent checked-Sage audit matched the expansion on **16,384 code assignments** across two frozen right-code slices; the public slice checks contained no root. This pilot establishes a concrete transfer cost and leaves the full public 34-bit block unresolved.
