# Proof map

Toolchain: `leanprover/lean4:v4.34.1`, pinned in `lean-toolchain`. `NoAnnihilator.lean` and `FieldIdentities.lean` built with `lean <module>.lean`; `lean.stdout` and `field_lean.stdout` record their `#print axioms` results. The checked declaration `InverseTraceANF.no_nonzero_annihilator_of_injective_eval` states that an injective evaluation map with `evaluate 0 = 0` has no nonzero coefficient vector with all-zero evaluations. It depends on no axioms. The failed initial file-path attempts are retained in the `*_v1.*` Lean outputs.

For each N131 slice, `annihilator_rank_v2.py` constructs the 794-column monomial evaluation matrix through degree four and records 794 eligible pivot codes. `audit_pivots.py` independently verifies those codes' field inverses by exponentiation, their traces, and their evaluation-row independence. Those finite computations supply the injectivity hypothesis for the concrete matrix; the specific 794-by-794 certificates are not kernel-reduced by Lean. The Lean formalization is therefore **partial** for the concrete N131 rank statement. The exact missing step is a Lean definition of the frozen affine slice, finite-field source eligibility, its 794 monomial evaluation matrix, and a kernel-checked replay of the recorded pivot elimination.

The checked declarations `InverseTraceANF.first_source_inverse_identity` and `second_source_inverse_identity` prove the two field identities under the exact nonzero and characteristic-two hypotheses. `InverseTraceANF.source_trace_conditions_iff` then proves equivalence of the source-witness trace tests and the two direct inverse-trace tests for any additive Boolean trace map. These declarations use only the standard Lean axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.

The Boolean Möbius transform and its inverse are checked over all bits by the producer but are not yet formalized. A Lean proof that the exact N41/N131 field trace implementations satisfy the abstract additivity hypothesis, and a kernel-checked replay of the finite rank certificates, remain open. Those bridges are explicit rather than substituted into the generic theorem's hypotheses.
