# Proof map: reduced second-product norm

| Result | Exact assumptions and statement | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reduction preserves a product at an annihilator root | In any commutative ring, if `∏ factors = remainder + divisorValue·quotient` and `divisorValue=0`, then `remainder=∏ factors`; equivalently their zero tests agree | `NormRoot.reduced_product_value` and `NormRoot.reduced_product_zero_iff` in [NormRoot.lean](NormRoot.lean), pinned Lean 4.34.1, checked in [lean_check.log](lean_check.log), `#print axioms` empty. |
| Linearized annihilator update preserves old roots and kills the new basis value | In a commutative ring, `Lnew(x)=Lold(x)²+αLold(x)`; old zero stays zero; when `Lold(v)=α` and `α+α=0`, the new value is zero | `NormRoot.annihilator_update_old_root` and `NormRoot.annihilator_update_new_basis` checked under pinned Lean. Axiom audit shows only standard foundations. The exact coefficient recurrence in Sage and the rank-seven span enumeration are checked computationally; a faithful Lean polynomial-construction bridge remains open. |
| Product is zero precisely when an included factor is zero | Integral-domain property of `GF(2^41)` and finite product | Follows by induction from no zero divisors. The checked theorem above establishes equality of reduced and ordinary product values at each third-span root. The concrete 16-factor products are independently replayed in [verification.json](verification.json). A field-level Lean definition for this exact binary extension remains open. |
| Coefficient occupancy and rank | Exact N41 field, target-conditioned 213-term tensor, frozen q prefix and annihilator | Measured by checked Sage in [result_v2.json](result_v2.json) and [result_v4.json](result_v4.json). An independent pure-Python [rank verifier](rank_verification.json) checks all 32,896 serialized coefficients, their 32,768 nonzeros, and full 128-column rank. There is no asserted mathematical lower bound on N131 density or circuit size. |
| Separated-term lower bound | Over `GF(2^41)`, a bivariate polynomial written as `Σᵢ Aᵢ(p)Bᵢ(r)` has coefficient-matrix rank at most its term count | Written proof in [RESULT.md](RESULT.md): each term is an outer product of coefficient vectors and has rank at most one; rank is subadditive. Together with the verified rank 128 this gives a bound of 128 separated terms for this exact N41 polynomial. A faithful Lean matrix-rank theorem remains open because the pinned bare `Std` project has no suitable finite-field matrix library. |
| Source-to-resultant bridge | Frozen N41 curve, shifted reciprocal, seven source pair states, public target, four fixed suffix states | The prior experiment's Sage group audit and full 40³ screen supply the finite certificate. A faithful Lean formalization of the curve-to-resultant implication remains open. |
