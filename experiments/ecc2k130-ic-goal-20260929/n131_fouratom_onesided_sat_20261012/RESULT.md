# Specialization shrinks N131 one-sided SAT, but the full product scan favors GCD

**Result.** Fixing the first N131 W15 product before Boolean encoding reduced the second-product formula from 104 MB to about 131 KB for a typical first product. The archived positive first product yielded a SAT model for second-product mask 3; every emitted CNF/XOR row and the original field eliminant checked. A separate first product and a frozen panel of 16 sampled first products returned UNSAT. The panel's median solver call was **159 ms** and median per-product specialization, emission, solve, and check was **205 ms**, plus **21.6 s** to construct the shared full ANF in the panel run. The archived one-sided GCD oracle completed its entire 16,384-first-product scan on this same constructed block output in **6.522 s** of internal query time. The direct specialized SAT branch therefore does not offer a useful replacement for that complete GCD scan under the measured implementation and inputs.

## Exact method and controls

The [pinned-formula protocol](protocol.json), [specialized protocol](specialized_protocol.json), and [panel protocol](panel_protocol.json) froze source and solver hashes, masks, one worker, 4-GiB sampled RSS ceiling, and 20-second or 5-second per-solver caps before each run. All cases use the archived constructed N131 W15 four-atom output over `GF(2)[t]/(t^131+t^13+t²+t+1)` with seed 13686 in both slots and rotations `(26,39)`. The 14-bit first-product mask is 5576 for the source control, 0 for a separate negative control, and the panel masks are listed in its protocol. The second product remains unpinned in every formula.

The first method appends 14 unit clauses to the inherited [full joint formula](../n41_n131_fouratom_product_anf_sat_20261012/RESULT.md) without changing its 220,535 variables or 14.4 million XOR literal occurrences. The specialized method instead substitutes the fixed first-product bits into the field-valued ANF, cancels equal second-product monomials over the binary field, and emits a new 14-primary-bit XCNF. The source-positive specialized ANF has 470 monomials and a 131,230-byte formula; first product zero has 106 monomials and a 23,809-byte formula. Each 16-mask panel case also had 470 monomials and a 130–132 KB formula.

The [panel aggregate](panel_result.json) records every mask, status, formula size, monomial count, and observed min/median/max. Its shared ANF build was 21.634 s; the separate two-control shared build was 17.932 s. The [source-positive specialized receipt](source_first_product.small.json) shows a complete model check and exact source-product match. Every other terminal row is retained in its own JSON and raw solver output. The two 104 MB pinned formulas are compressed archives; receipt `formula.sha256` and `formula.bytes` refer to their uncompressed XCNF streams. The [archive audit](archive_audit.json) checks all 20 formula sizes and raw-stream hashes against their receipts.

The GCD reference is the frozen [rotated four-atom inverse](../n131_w15_rotated_fouratom_inverse_20261010/RESULT.md): it visits all 16,384 first-product values, with a polynomial GCD against the complete second-product image for each. Its 6.522 s is an internal query interval; the SAT times above include process launch and formula parse. These CPU observations came from an unisolated host and remain exploratory stage measurements. Multiplying the panel median by 16,384 would be an extrapolation, not a measured full scan. The one-sided SAT runner does not generate the offered output from an ordinary public target. `candidate_id` remains null.

## Next algorithmic gate

The product specialization is useful as an exact diagnostic for a few chosen first products, while GCD remains the measured complete family filter. A further candidate needs a target-derived necessary condition that discards many first products *before* either per-product GCD or SAT work. The cheapest falsifier is to derive such a condition on the frozen N41 unpinned public-point relation, measure its rejection rate and cost on both positive and field controls, and then pair it with the archived N131 W15 GCD scan on the identical output. The complete single-target IC pipeline still requires target-derived offered outputs, relation rank, descent, replay, and a same-point rho comparison.
