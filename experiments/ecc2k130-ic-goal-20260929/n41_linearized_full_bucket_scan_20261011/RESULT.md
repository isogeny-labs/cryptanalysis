# Exact inverse join completes the eight raw N41 bucket-family controls

An exact target-conditioned S3 inverse join classified **all eight frozen raw residual lifts** while both W6 pair seeds and pair-output coordinates were free. It found one family hit, on positive lift 3 at left seed 16, right seed 5, and left reciprocal `585190544792`; the other seven lifts had zero coordinate hits. The independent checked-Sage replay verified the positive raw group sum and the selected seed-bucket panel. The inverse join uses **2,046 quadratic roots per lift** after building reusable 1,984-coordinate maps for each side. It replaces the inner 63-right-seed loop of the direct linearized scan for this exact N41 domain.

The field is `GF(2)[t]/(t^41+t³+1)` on `y²+xy=x³+1`, curve `EC1N41Ckb1h7d5e80d3c4d0`. The source uses W6 rotations 20 and 30, actual projected base size **B=256**, and 26 folded columns. The [frozen targets](targets.json) are the four raw preimages of each previously fixed positive and negative projected residual. Each raw query uses one worker, a 120-second cap, and a 4-GiB process RSS ceiling. These are two-free-pair component queries with `candidate_id: null`; the known positive residual is a correctness control, not an ordinary N131 relation-yield sample.

For each nonzero source seed `s`, the pair product `p=a²+sa` runs over a five-dimensional binary image. Its transformed output `Z` satisfies `(1+p)Z²+pZ+u_s²=0`. The direct [linearized scan](run.py) constructs every left output and, at fixed left `Z` and target `t`, checks all 63 right seeds using the exact five-vector span predicate from the [earlier derivation](../n41_linearized_bucket_composition_20261011/RESULT.md). It completed seven lifts; the eighth reached its cap after 90,216 of 124,992 seed decisions. A separately frozen repeat of that eighth lift also reached its cap after 78,498 decisions. Both censored rows are preserved.

The [inverse join](inverse_join.py) first builds both complete unfiltered pair-output maps. At fixed left `Z` and target `t`, put `C=(Z²+Z)(t²+t)` and `D=(Z+t)²`. It solves `(1+C)R²+CR+D=0` for at most two right outputs `R`, then looks up their seed labels. The linear exceptional case `C=1` is handled explicitly. This enumerates compatible right coordinates only after the public-target condition, and a map hit retains every seed label. The [protocol](inverse_protocol.json) froze code and inputs before the run. The output maps are target-independent setup; root solving and lookups are charged to each target-dependent query.

The deterministic change is 124,992 seed-span decisions per complete direct lift versus 2,046 target-conditioned roots per inverse lift, with exact matching outcomes on the seven common complete rows.

## Challenge-width gate

For W14 at N131, the literal unfiltered left construction has up to `(2^14−1)·2^14 = 268,419,072` seed/product factors before coordinate deduplication. A direct per-left-output, per-right-seed scan would visit up to `4,397,509,656,576` family decisions per raw lift; this is an upper work envelope for that implementation, not a measured N131 count. The inverse join removes the right-seed loop but still needs a target-compatible **left-output generator** and a right-output membership structure. Enumerating a large left domain independently of the target leaves the main ECC2K-130 search problem in place. The next gate is a joint target-derived proposal for left block outputs whose complete source-bound work, preparation, memory, and ordinary relation yield fit the one-worker 600-second/4-GiB N131 envelope.
