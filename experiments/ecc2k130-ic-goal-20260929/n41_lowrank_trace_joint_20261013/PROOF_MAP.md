Write `c=Σαᵢuᵢ`, `d=Σβⱼvⱼ`. In characteristic two, squaring is
additive. Expand `(tcd)²+tcd+c²+d²+t²` and collect constant, left,
right, and cross terms. Applying the additive trace after multiplication
by `λ` gives the displayed Boolean bilinear equation in `RESULT.md`.
Every exact S3 zero satisfies every trace cut. An omitted cross row has
all coefficients zero, so its contribution vanishes for every right
coordinate; `TraceRows.forced_zero_prefix` checks that structural step.

For the N131 dimension bound, the map from the 131 multiplier bits to
the `k·s` selected cross coefficients is binary-linear. Rank-nullity
gives kernel dimension at least `131−k·s`. With `k=7,s=18`, this is at
least five. Any multiplier in that kernel has seven zero rows; the row
span of the full 18-row cross matrix is generated by its remaining at
most eleven rows. This is a conditional fixed-family construction. It
does not give a compact representation of all product-family pairs or
guarantee twelve mutually informative cuts on every N131 pair.

Build `TraceRows.lean` with `lean TraceRows.lean` in this directory. The
source uses bundled Std, has no `sorry`, `admit`, research axiom, or
native-evaluation proof, and `lean_check.log` records the kernel axiom
audit. A faithful Lean theorem for the exact N131 finite-field expansion
and rank-nullity bridge remains open; the independent Sage controls
verify the concrete N41 panel only. `lean4checker --fresh` is unavailable
in this environment.
