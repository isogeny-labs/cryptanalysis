For an even point `P=(x,y)`, choose `λ` with `λ²+λ=x`. If `2R=P`, the binary-curve doubling formula gives `x(R)²=y+(λ+1)x`. Thus `P` is divisible by four exactly when `Tr(y+(λ+1)x)=0`; the choice of `λ` does not change the trace because `Tr(x)=0`. For an odd point, add the rational order-four point `(1,0)`, classify the resulting even point, and subtract that point's class. This is the independent auditor's field formula. The exact curve/doubling bridge is checked computationally here and remains open in Lean.
