The central mathematical implication has a short proof: the affine span of a set is the anchor plus the linear span of its differences, so each exact member lies in it. The raw point check then resolves the two signs sharing an `x` coordinate. The producer uses high-bit Gaussian elimination on integer coordinate encodings; the independent Sage audit uses low-bit elimination, Sage elliptic-curve arithmetic, and a separate `GF(2)` rank check of every two-code family.

No new abstract theorem is asserted by the 33-panel count.
