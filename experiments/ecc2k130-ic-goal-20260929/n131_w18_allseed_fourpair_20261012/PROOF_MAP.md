For points with nonzero x-coordinate, put `a=1/x(P)`, `b=1/x(Q)`, and `c=1/x(P+Q)`. Eliminating the two y coordinates from `y²+xy=x³+1` gives the reciprocal S3 identity `(1+(ab)²)c²+(ab)c+(a+b)²=0`. Substituting the diagonal-source reciprocals `a=w_a`, `b=w_b` yields the pair equation. Applying the same identity to each balanced internal sum yields the three upper links. The equations are necessary on the nonexceptional chart; a returned model still undergoes explicit source-mask, column, point-sign, and group-sum replay before it can enter the relation matrix.
