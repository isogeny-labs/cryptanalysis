# The joint resultant is already implied by the existing S3 links

The exact target-conditioned quadratic resultant adds no algebraic
filter to the N41 five-pair circuit once its two existing S3 links hold.
The implication is proved over every characteristic-two field in
`ResultantRedundancy.lean`. An independent Sage calculation reduces
the resultant to zero in the ideal generated by the two links over
`GF(2)[z,u,v,w,t]`.

## Method and result

The circuit links a preceding partial sum and a final pair through
`S3(z,u,w)=0` and `S3(w,v,t)=0`, where `t` is the fixed public target
reciprocal. The candidate extra gate eliminates a witness `y` from
`S3(u,v,y)=0` and `S3(z,t,y)=0`. Its polynomial is

```
R = (AG+CD)^2 + (AE+BD)(BG+CE)
A = 1+(uv)^2, B = uv, C = (u+v)^2
D = 1+(zt)^2, E = zt, G = (z+t)^2.
```

The frozen universal check found `R ∈ ⟨S3(z,u,w), S3(w,v,t)⟩` with
zero Gröbner remainder in 0.039 s. The Lean theorem covers all values,
including exceptional leading coefficients. This explains why the
resultant's positive panel value is a correctness control but not
evidence of additional filtering after the existing links.

The prior source-quadratic reduction preserved all 33 N41 panel values
but expanded the resultant from 23 to 54 polynomial terms. Its circuit
would therefore need a solver-specific propagation benefit to justify
the extra algebra. The universal implication gives no such filtering
benefit, so the reduced circuit was not built.

## Fixed-budget solver observation

The preceding frozen ABBA probe compared the explicit right-sum witness
with the direct resultant on the same N41 public point, one worker,
20,000 conflict cap per arm, and a 4 GiB cap. Each arm ended
`INDETERMINATE_CONFLICT_CAP` at 20,002 reported conflicts.

| Arm | Decisions | Propagations | Wall seconds | Outcome |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Explicit right-sum witness, two repetitions | 35,089 | 79M | 2.526, 2.509 | Conflict cap |
| Direct resultant, two repetitions | 45,271 | 38M | 1.280, 1.271 | Conflict cap |

The direct resultant used fewer propagations and less capped wall time,
but 29% more decisions. It failed the predeclared decision promotion
criterion. These values are stage diagnostics on an ordinary host,
not single-target online IC/rho measurements. `candidate_id` remains
`null`; the code has not passed the end-to-end pipeline gates.

## Next exact constraint

The next design must expose information absent from the two S3 links.
One concrete route is to bind each pair's chosen reciprocal root to
its actual signed source-point sum, then test a joint target condition
that rejects inconsistent sign choices. The first gate is a proof of
branch correctness for the 71 admissible source codes on N41; the
second is a frozen-panel and bounded public-query measurement of
rejection and circuit cost. This would test whether source-point data
adds useful target-conditioned pruning rather than another implied
polynomial consequence of the x-only links.
