# Proof map

## Universal redundancy theorem

`ResultantRedundancy.joint_resultant_of_existing_links` in
`ResultantRedundancy.lean` states that, for every field `F` satisfying
`∀ x : F, x + x = 0` and every `z u v w t : F`, the two equations
`S3(z,u,w)=0` and `S3(w,v,t)=0` imply that the quadratic resultant of
`S3(u,v,y)` and `S3(z,t,y)` vanishes. No nonzero leading-coefficient,
distinctness, rational-root, subgroup, or curve-point hypothesis is used.

The readable proof is polynomial ideal membership. Let

```
S3(a,b,c) = (1 + (ab)^2)c^2 + abc + (a+b)^2
R = (AG+CD)^2 + (AE+BD)(BG+CE)
A = 1+(uv)^2, B = uv, C = (u+v)^2
D = 1+(zt)^2, E = zt, G = (z+t)^2.
```

Expansion over `GF(2)[z,u,v,w,t]` puts `R` in the ideal generated by
`S3(z,u,w)` and `S3(w,v,t)`. The checked Lean proof derives the same
universal implication directly from the two equations by polynomial
normalization (`grind +ring`). The Sage ideal calculation is an independent
computational check, not an assumption of the Lean theorem.

Toolchain: `leanprover/lean4:v4.34.1`, pinned in `lean-toolchain`.
Build command: `lean ResultantRedundancy.lean` in this directory.
The output in `lean_check.txt` reports only the standard Lean foundations
`propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. The theorem source contains
no `sorry`, `admit`, custom research axiom, or native proof evaluation.
`lean4checker --fresh` was unavailable on this host.

## Empirical connection

The theorem applies to the exact extra resultant gate tested in the
frozen N41 circuit. It proves that this gate cannot eliminate any
assignment satisfying both pre-existing S3 links as field equations.
It does not establish equivalence of the two Boolean encodings under a
bounded SAT search: their propagation and branching behavior differs.
The ABBA comparison is recorded in the source experiment's
`probe_result.json` and summarized in `RESULT.md`.
