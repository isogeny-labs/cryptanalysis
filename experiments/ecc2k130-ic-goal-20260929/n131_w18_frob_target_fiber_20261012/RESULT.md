# Frobenius target conditioning expands N131 relation support at fixed matrix width

One W18 terminal-fiber circuit now tests **all 131 Frobenius conjugates of the same public point** and the four signs of one fixed terminal edge. Pulling a relation for `τ^jQ` back by `τ^(131−j)` keeps every factor-base point in the full signed-Frobenius closure and keeps its folded column. The exact closure has **34,339,816 usable points before folding and 131,068 columns**. The 131-choice expansion raises the labeled uniform raw-point source ceiling from `141.609150594` to **`18,550.7987278`** while keeping the 32-state circuit's **195,294 AND gates**. The frozen ordinary query searched for 600.040 seconds and ended `BOUNDED_UNKNOWN`; its complete formula and all 524 residuals passed independent replay.

The [source table](../n131_w16_w18_sixpair_width_gate_20261010/support_w18.tsv) has 131,307 rational masks and 131,068 signed-Frobenius columns. The prior checked-Sage [W18 geometry audit](../n131_lowpower_w18_m8/audit.json) establishes its orbit partition on the same field and curve model. On the prime-order subgroup, Frobenius acts by an element of order 131; since `−1` is outside that odd-order cyclic subgroup, every nonidentity column expands to 262 distinct signed conjugates. Thus `131,068 × 262 = 34,339,816` is the actual full-closure base size. The source ceiling is **131 times the exact four-sign labeled tuple count** divided by the full curve order `4r`; different `j` tuple families may overlap, so it is an upper ceiling rather than an observed fixed-point relation count. This experiment changes the factor-base policy from the earlier fixed-window union, while preserving its folded column count.
