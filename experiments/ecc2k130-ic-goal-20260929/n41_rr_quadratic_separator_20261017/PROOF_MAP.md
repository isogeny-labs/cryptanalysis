The theorem module has no `sorry`, `admit`, custom claim axioms, or native-evaluation proof bypasses. The file is built with `leanprover/lean4:v4.34.1`; `lean_check.stdout` records `#print axioms` for all four declarations. Formalization is partial at the two structural bridges listed above. The exact numerical certificate identity is independently replayed coefficient by coefficient over the full 41-bit and 131-bit Boolean input domains.
