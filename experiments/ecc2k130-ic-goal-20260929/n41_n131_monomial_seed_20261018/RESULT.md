# Monomial half-trace seed selection for exact W9 and W29 five-pair queries

The seed rule `w=h²+h` with `h=t^k` gives a sparse half-trace root and a smaller exact N131 W29 three-plus-two SAT circuit. A frozen ten-seed sweep selected `k=5`: 75,869 AND gates and 229,459 clauses on the same public N131 point, versus 76,859 and 232,429 for the earlier seed. An exact fixed-prefix source census retained 261,996 eligible pairs among 1,048,576 canonical masks, and an independent Sage census matched every count. The N41 companion source yielded an independently replayed ten-leaf relation on a previously generated public point. The N131 public prefix-3 branch reached its frozen 600-second cap with `BOUNDED_UNKNOWN`; `terminal_result.json` independently reconstructs the branch formula and checks the retained solver stream.

## Seed identity and query method

For odd extension degree `d=2n+1` in characteristic two, write `HT(x)=Σ_{i=0}^{n}x^(4^i)` and `Tr(h)=Σ_{j=0}^{d-1}h^(2^j)`. If `Tr(h)=0`, then `HT(h²+h)=h`, because the half-trace terms expand to all `d+1` consecutive Frobenius powers of `h`, and `h^(2^d)=h`. Thus choosing `h=t^k`, when its trace vanishes, fixes `H=h²` and `C=1+H` in the exact pair-output equation `C z² + p(a)z + H=0`. The explicit N41 seed is `w=t⁶+t³` (mask 36, `k=3`); the N131 sweep uses `w=t^(2k)+t^k` for `5≤k≤14`. `MonomialHalfTrace.lean` proves the generic odd-degree implication; `PROOF_MAP.md` states the exact assumptions and the field-instance boundary.

The query couples five signed pair outputs through four S3 links, with three pairs on one side of the target split and two on the other. The N41 native join instead indexes two left pair sums and probes them against one middle and two right pair sums across the Frobenius orbit. These are component experiments; `candidate_id` is null because factor-base log collection, rank, linear algebra, target recovery, and the complete pipeline are not specified or measured here.

The N41 public subgroup point is `[339942650206,165641111719]`, with raw cofactor preimage `[1476947961888,1253336411133]` over `GF(2^41)` defined by `t^41+t^3+1`, on `y²+xy=x³+1`. The N131 public subgroup point is `[1052727368764798954359115163823647684025,2071227447786120917960296877345506689833]`, with raw preimage `[877610862732227974378969808549299291499,2063336771837286479337213121437715536874]` over `GF(2^131)` defined by `t^131+t^13+t²+t+1`, on the same curve model. These exact points were frozen before the seed sweep.

The N131 circuit sweep examined all ten `k` values from 5 through 14 on one frozen public point, with exact `H`, `C`, source-inverse gates, and all four S3 links. Seed `k=5` minimized AND gates and clauses in that set. The corresponding prefix census examined 1,048,576 canonical masks per seed, with two exact field inversions and trace checks per mask. The `k=5` source count is 202 above the earlier seed's fixed-prefix count; this is a geometric diagnostic, not an ordinary-query relation yield. The constructed circuit control is a correctness check and was completed after the public SAT search had launched.

The first N41 mask-36 join attempt ended `PRODUCER_FAILURE` in 0.295 s because the inherited native input check still required mask 56. Its original source, binary, protocol, stdout, stderr, and result are preserved. `join_2p1_v2.cpp` changes only that seed check, and `join_protocol_v2.json` freezes the corrected attempt before execution.

The N131 branch formula occupied 6,176,346 B after eight prefix unit clauses, versus 7,425,375 B in the earlier branch. The verified solver stream ended without a terminal SAT or UNSAT status under the parent wall cap; its last flushed restart row was 3,008 with approximately 3.507 million reported conflicts. The earlier branch logged 3,505 restart rows in its own 600-second cap. These are censored search-progress observations on an ordinary shared host, not a solver-speed ranking.

The N41 join parent wall includes native input checking and the target-conditioned indexed search after the precomputed source is ready; it is a stage diagnostic. The CPU host was not isolated under the repository's Tier 0 or Tier 1 comparison gates. The changed seed and the first-hit orientation also differ, so the 9.423 s and 22.987 s observations are not an online speedup estimate.
