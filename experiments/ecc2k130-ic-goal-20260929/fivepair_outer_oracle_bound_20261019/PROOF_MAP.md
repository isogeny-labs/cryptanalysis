## Readable counting proof

Let `R` be the set of possible projected right two-pair sums, with `|R|≤M`. For a fixed outer triple sum `a`, successful subgroup targets lie in the translate `a+R`, whose cardinality is at most `M`. For one target-advice value with at most `k` fixed outer proposals, the union of their translates has at most `kM` elements. Across at most `C` advice values, the union has at most `CkM` elements. Dividing by the prime subgroup order `r` gives `Pr[success]≤min(1,CkM/r)` for a uniform subgroup target. A no-hit-only adaptive sequence is covered because its sequence of proposals before the first hit is the fixed all-negative path for that advice value.

For the W29 source, each right slot has at most `2^28` canonical masks and four signed pair outputs per mask, so `S≤2^30`. The two rotated slots may differ, so the safe ordered-pair ceiling is `M≤S²=2^60`; an unordered-pair binomial ceiling is not used. Substituting this `M` and the exact N131 subgroup order into the structural inequality gives the rigorous threshold. The sample-projection calculation substitutes a smaller conditional `S=4·68,308,992` and keeps that condition explicit.

The Lean structural theorem and new numeric lemmas are kernel-checked, but there is no single Lean declaration connecting the concrete elliptic-curve source, the exact N41 support census, subgroup projection, and the uniform input law. The finite-instance source and group bridges are preserved by the frozen checked-Sage and native receipts. `lean4checker --fresh` is unavailable on this host.
