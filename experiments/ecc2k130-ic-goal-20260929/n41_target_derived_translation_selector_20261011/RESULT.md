# Target residual bits do not concentrate cross-block translations

On the frozen N41 twelve-column relation-source panel, a target-derived seven-proposal selector for either full-width cross-block translation performed **worse than the seed-conditional frequency baseline**. With the two free pairs subtracted from each public sum, the first translation was included in 1,156 of 2,954 held-out generating rows, versus 1,345 for the baseline; the second was included in 1,139 versus 1,310. Independent reconstruction of source masks and a separate likelihood evaluation matched all six model outcomes. This specific coordinate-bit model therefore supplies no concentration for the W18 target-derived translation idea.

The source is `y²+xy=x³+1` over `GF(2)[t]/(t^41+t³+1)`, curve `EC1N41Ckb1h023d9a38bbf7`, with **B=384** usable projected points and **K=26** signed-Frobenius columns. The six W6 pair rotations are `0,5,10,15,20,25`. The [archived generator](../n41_w6_public_seed_predictor_20261011/RESULT.md) supplied 100,000 exact twelve-column group sums, with two free pair slots and two constrained four-atom blocks; the [residual record](../n41_prefix_residual_seed_selector_20261011/RESULT.md) supplies `Q`, `Q−P₀`, and `Q−P₀−P₁` on those identical rows. This experiment retains only the **14,781 rows where the two four-atom blocks have the same nonzero six-bit seed**, so that corresponding source pairs differ by a translation `u`. The original first-80,000/final-20,000 split is preserved before this filter, leaving 11,827 training and 2,954 held-out rows.

For a corresponding unordered source pair `(a,a+s)` and `(b,b+s)`, the equivalent translations are `u=a+b` and `u+s`. The label is `min(u,u+s)`, separately for the first and second corresponding pair slots. The [extractor and fitter](run.py) rebuild the exact pair-state order and check both translated mask sets on every selected row. The baseline takes the seven most frequent translations for the supplied block seed `s` in training. The model keeps that conditional seed prior and adds Laplace-smoothed Bernoulli likelihoods for 41 `x` and 41 `y` bits from one of three points: `Q`, `Q−P₀`, or `Q−P₀−P₁`. Conditioning on the generating seed gives both arms more information than a public-only selector would have; the experiment asks whether residual bits add useful ranking once that seed is available.

| Held-out generating translation | Seed-conditional frequency | Public `Q` bits | After one free pair | After two free pairs |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| First corresponding pair | **1,345 / 2,954** | 1,164 | 1,196 | 1,156 |
| Second corresponding pair | **1,310 / 2,954** | 1,120 | 1,106 | 1,139 |

For the two-free-pair residual, the paired recall differences from the baseline are **−6.398 percentage points** for the first translation (95% interval −8.185 to −4.611) and **−5.789 points** for the second (−7.655 to −3.922). The exact discordance counts and McNemar values for all six comparisons are in [result.json](result.json). These intervals describe only the declared generated-relation law and held-out split. The result is not an ordinary-target relation-yield estimate: a point can have other valid witnesses besides its generating row.

The [protocol](protocol.json) froze the source input, both archived row files and protocols, model, split, one worker, 120 seconds, and 1-GiB process RSS before the successful run. Its total local interval was 2.670 s with 162,152,448 B peak RSS; no CPU speed ratio is inferred on this unisolated host. The [independent audit](audit.json) re-parses the original atom source, reconstructs the pair translations and held-out label digests, and scores all six arms as `x log p + (1−x) log(1−p)` rather than the producer's log-odds contraction. It reports `PASS_HELDOUT_TRANSLATION_REPLAY`.

The first frozen [source](run_v1.py), [protocol](protocol_v1.json), and [receipt](result_v1.json) are retained. NumPy's matrix-product implementation emitted floating-point warnings even though its score matrix was finite. The version-two source uses an explicit tensor contraction; a direct numerical check found maximum absolute score difference about `1.1×10⁻¹⁴`, and the two receipts have identical outcome tables. The independent audit reproduces the version-two outcomes without either contraction.

## N131 design decision

The [W18 translation support census](../n131_crossblock_product_offset_20261011/RESULT.md) shows that choosing a small translation set without the target loses too much source density. This N41 panel finds that public or free-pair-residual coordinate bits do not rescue a seven-translation budget under the tested model, even when the generating seed is supplied. A viable target-derived translation method must use exact S3 and product-space constraints **jointly with unresolved block outputs**, preserve substantially broader W18 translation support, and show an unpinned N41 relation plus a source-bound 600-second/4-GiB N131 work gate before an ordinary challenge-point query. This is proposal `Q20261011213500`; `candidate_id` remains null.
