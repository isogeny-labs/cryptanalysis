# A complete N41 product-span two-output census

**The product-span relaxation compresses 130,405 possible pair coordinates into 2,048 linear systems, and a target-conditioned S3 join reduces them to three exact constructed-target pairs.** The selected four distinct W6 source masks pass checked-Sage group replay with two sign assignments. The same complete relaxed-output join finds zero algebraic pairs at the frozen N41 public point in this four-point, single-rotation source slice. This measures a strong target filter while identifying explicit output enumeration as the N131 transfer barrier.

## Exact source and relaxed equations

The field is `GF(2)[t]/(t^41+t³+1)`, with curve `y²+xy=x³+1` and curve ID `EC1N41Ckb1h023d9a38bbf7`. The source is all **32** rational nonzero `x=mask*t` in `W6=span(t,…,t^6)`. Its 496 unordered distinct-mask pairs have **991** distinct `c=1/x(P+Q)` coordinates, retaining both quadratic roots. This is a point-decomposition component with `candidate_id: null`; no complete IC candidate or usable-base count is inferred from these raw coordinates.

For source coordinates `a,b∈W6`, let `s=a+b` and `p=ab`. The exact pair equation is `(1+p²)c²+pc+s²=0`. The relaxation keeps `s∈W6` but allows any `p∈D=span(t²,…,t^12)`, an 11-dimensional product span. Its odd-degree halftrace form is a **42-row homogeneous GF(2) system in 48 variables** `(c,s,ε)` for each fixed `p`, including the trace guard. The [native enumerator](native.cpp) exhausts all `2^11` products and every kernel vector. It found nullity six for 2,026 products and seven for 22 products: **132,480** linear assignments yield **130,405** distinct nonzero coordinates, an expansion of **131.589×** over the exact source-output set. Every one of the 991 exact outputs is present.

In the same `c=1/x` chart, the target link is the symmetric polynomial `S3(c,d,t)=(cdt)²+cdt+c²+d²+t²`. The [checked-Sage symbolic identity](s3_identity.json) derives it by clearing denominators in the repository's `1/(x+1)` S3 polynomial. For each relaxed `c`, the [native join](native_join.cpp) solves the quadratic in `d` and probes the relaxed-output set. The constructed target law takes the first root of `S3(c₀,c₁,t)=0` for the first two sorted exact outputs; that law was fixed before the joined run. The four source masks were selected afterward for independent group replay. The public control uses `t=1/x_Q` for `Q=(920359962848,755404565011)`.

## Cost and transfer

The [frozen protocol](protocol.json) binds the native and audit sources, binary hashes, source-output file, relaxed-output file, target law, one-worker execution, 300-second cap, and 4-GiB memory cap. The native enumeration took **0.678 seconds** of monitored runner wall and sampled **9.04 MB** peak RSS. The final joined two-target run took **31.379 seconds** of runner wall and sampled **11.40 MB** peak RSS. Those are combined stage diagnostics on an unisolated host; they are not single-target online timings. The [raw native](native_receipt.json) and [join](join_receipt.json) receipts retain the output hashes and actual caps. The checked Sage runtime was saved before the group job.

The literal W18 N131 transfer has `2^35` product-span elements. Each fixed-product system has 150 binary variables and at most 132 equations, hence nullity at least 18 and at least `2^18` assignments. An exhaustive product-first enumerator therefore visits **at least `2^53 = 9,007,199,254,740,992` assignments**. To fit 600 seconds it would need more than **1.50×10^13 assignments/s**, before the public-target join or any exact source checks. The [machine-readable bound](transfer_bound.json) states its scope. This is a representation-specific lower bound on the explicit enumerator, not a bound on implicit elimination. The next test must compose the target S3 relation with these linear spaces without enumerating their coordinates, and retain several other pair blocks free on the unpinned N41 public point before any N131 promotion.
