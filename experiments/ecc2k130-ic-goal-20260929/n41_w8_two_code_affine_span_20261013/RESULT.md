# Two-code W8 outputs have affine rank seven and a nonzero cubic parity

The frozen N41 W8 source yields a concrete structural result for a target-conditioned search: all 1,554 admissible fixed-code pairs in its first two slots produce eight distinct output coordinates of affine rank seven in both the ordinary and reciprocal charts. Their affine hull has 128 values, a 16-fold relaxation of exact eight-value membership. The XOR of each eight-value set is nonzero, so a bijective three-bit branch encoding of either coordinate has Boolean degree three. These claims were independently replayed with checked Sage and the general XOR implication has a checked Lean proof; [PROOF_MAP.md](PROOF_MAP.md) gives the exact bridge and its limits.

The curve is `EC1N41Ckb1h7d5e80d3c4d0` over `GF(2)[t]/(t^41+t³+1)`, with `y²+xy=x³+1`, subgroup order `549756390943`, and cofactor four. The point is the archived ordinary public input `Q=(1458168294740,1672312028033)` from scalar seed `202609291700`. W8 seed 56 gives 40 product codes per slot, four signed pair states per code; 46 of the 1,600 ordered first-slot/second-slot code pairs share folded columns and are excluded. The source and checked Sage runtime are hash-bound by the [frozen census protocol](protocol.json) and [runtime receipt](sage_runtime_info.json). This is a component study with `candidate_id: null`.

The census used one worker with a 180-second and 4-GiB cap. Its recorded wall interval was 6.161 s and sampled process peak 27.7 MB; the independent Sage replay took 36.878 s and sampled 225.1 MB. The target panel took 0.740 s and its independent Sage replay 6.914 s. These CPU times are exploratory diagnostics on an unisolated host. The target panel computes residuals only after all five suffix states are supplied. It therefore measures conditional discrimination and does not measure whole-family rejection while those suffix choices remain free. Its 64 random suffixes are a fixed diagnostic panel, not a natural relation-yield sample.

The first target-panel producer stopped on an assertion comparing a list code pair with tuple code pairs. Its [failure receipt](target_failure_v1.json) and [diagnosis](failure_diagnosis_v1.json) are preserved; the corrected version was frozen and independently audited. The N131 table figures apply to the literal uncompressed enumeration only; the N131 rank, output multiplicity, and any implicit representation still need measurement.

The next experiment should keep at least one more product block free while applying the public target constraint. A practical falsifier is an exact three-block N41 predicate that rejects code triples before enumerating their `4³` raw sign combinations, paired with an independent witness-preservation check and a measured count of rejected versus retained triples. Its source-bound W20 projection must fit one worker, 600 seconds, and 4 GiB before an N131 ordinary-point attempt. [verify.py](verify.py) checks the frozen inputs, numerical receipts, failure preservation, and proof logs without rerunning the expensive Sage audits.
