For the cubic statement, write each output as `a xor dᵢ` except the anchor `a`. The anchor occurs eight times in the XOR of all outputs and cancels. The remainder is `d₀ xor … xor d₆`, which is nonzero by the independence hypothesis applied to the all-one subset. In the ANF of a three-bit vector function, Mobius inversion gives the cubic coefficient as the XOR of the values at all eight inputs. A bijective relabeling permutes those values and leaves the XOR unchanged. For the affine-parity statement, an affine functional constant on a set vanishes on every pairwise difference and therefore on their linear span; full affine span forces its linear part to vanish everywhere.
