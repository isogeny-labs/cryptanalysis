# Full E coordinates give a rank certificate through pole weight 24

The normalized B coordinate has no rational-coefficient identity of the
form \(A(x_C,y_C,x_E,y_E)x_B+B(x_C,y_C,x_E,y_E)=0\) within the frozen
pole-weight-24 space. The coefficient matrix has 605 columns and full
rank over both GF(997) and GF(1009). Because the monomial spaces are
nested, this certificate covers every smaller weight bound in the same
linear-in-\(x_B\) ansatz, including odd bounds. The result uses the
**full E point**, matching the coordinates that distinguish all 1,800
p997 rational source records in the preceding census.

## Frozen functions and screen

The [protocol](protocol.json) fixes
\(C=27a1:y_C^2+y_C=x_C^3-7\),
\(B=432a1:y_B^2=x_B^3-16\), and
\(E:y_E^2=x_E^3+432\), with the exact dual degree-three map

\[
x_E=\frac{x_B^3-64}{x_B^2},\qquad
y_E=\frac{y_B(x_B^3+128)}{x_B^3}.
\]

For both A and B, monomials are
\(x_C^a y_C^b x_E^c y_E^d\), with
\(b,d\in\{0,1\}\) and pole weight \(2a+3b+2c+3d\). A has weight
at most D; B has weight at most D+2. The screen uses q-series precision
1150, training exponents \(-(D+2),\ldots,900\), and holdout exponents
901–980. The smallest evaluated series precision was 1147.

| Bound D | Columns | Rank mod 997 | Pivot determinant mod 997 | Rank mod 1009 | Pivot determinant mod 1009 |

All six training and extended kernels have dimension zero.
`screen_result.json` retains exact monomial orders, row ranges, pivot
rows, ranks, and determinants; `qseries_modp.json` retains input
coefficients through q^1050. The [independent replay](verification.json)
reconstructs every Laurent monomial without Sage linear algebra,
checks the C, B, and E equations and both isogeny-coordinate identities
through q^980, and reproduces every determinant.

## Proof map and next calculation

Each matrix entry is a p-integral rational q-series coefficient. A
nonzero determinant modulo either prime therefore certifies a nonzero
rational minor. The rational coefficient map is injective at D=24;
zero-padding embeds every lower-weight coefficient space in that map.
The inherited checked Lean theorem
[`DirectLiftRank.no_relation_of_nonzero_minor`](../hecke-432-direct-lift-20261025/formal/DirectLiftRank.lean)
formalizes the nonzero-minor implication. The concrete q-series and
pivot determinants have independent computational replay; encoding
those numerical certificates in Lean remains a separate proof step.

This bound directs the next work to the generic function field of the
paired map \(X_0(432)\to X_0(27)\times E\). Its degree decides whether
the B coordinate belongs to the field generated by both C and E
coordinates. If it does, divisor and pole data can guide a much
smaller exact rational-function reconstruction than an unrestricted
high-weight monomial search.
