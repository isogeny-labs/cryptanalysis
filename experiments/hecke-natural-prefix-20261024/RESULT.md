**Contribution.** The trace-64 seed and its **first complete degree-2/3 Hecke frontier** construct a 1,760-column orbit table whose logs are uniquely determined by the verified Hecke rows, generator anchor, and internal shifts of at most three generator steps. A fresh checked-Sage run regenerated all sources, 1,506 accepted rows, and the same 1,760 columns from the source law; the radius-three shift quotient has **312 components and rank 312 over `GF(4021)`**. Its solved logs scalar-replay, and a separately frozen public target point recovers scalar **876** by direct table lookup. This is a substantially earlier preparation boundary than the six-frontier 3,949-column table.

The curve is `X₀(11): y²+y=x³−x²−10x−20` over `F_20023`. Quotienting by the order-five point `(5,5)` gives a subgroup generated by `G=5(1,8630)` of order `4,021`. The [frozen protocol](protocol.json) evaluates all defined-`j` points of absolute Frobenius trace 64, then all five torsion translates of each seed orbit in the first frontier. It accepts a degree-2/3 row only when every neighbor is rational with defined `j` and both the modular-polynomial and point-sum identities check.
