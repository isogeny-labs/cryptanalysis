The threshold is trained on completed D79 conductor-four classes: p63317 has base-plus-direct rank 37 and reaches rank 39 after Hecke rows; p68437 has rank 33 and ends at 34; p78157 has rank 32 and ends at 33. Freeze the rule **predict rank-39 reachability iff base-plus-direct rank modulo the largest prime-order factor is at least 37**. This is an empirical gate, not a theorem or a security claim.

Starting at odd `v=281`, scan through `v=501` by two with `p=v²+316`. Keep prime p if `5 | #E(F_p)`, `5 ∤ p−1`, and the largest prime divisor of `#E(F_p)` is at least 53, for `E: y²+y=x³−x²−10x−20`. Construct up to the first eight eligible D79 classes with the pinned degree-2/5 source and choose the first with exactly 40 points. Retain failed classes; stop `NO_CLASS` after the cap. Enumerate direct signed point relations at support at most four and compute the base-plus-direct rank over the recorded largest prime field. Seal the selected class, direct rows, rank and gate decision before any degree-13 lift.

Regardless of the gate decision, lift all 40 sources in ascending point-index order using the pinned degree-13 cache. Record every source status and elapsed lift interval. Enumerate signed cancellations among the 40 outside-class totals at support at most four, form and group-verify every Hecke row, and compute the full relation rank over the same prime field. A censored source leaves the validation outcome unknown. For completed sources, score the frozen prediction against the rank-39 criterion, retaining both success and failure. The all-source challenge is deliberately charged even when the gate says skip, so the gate can be falsified.

The direct rank is available before degree-13 work. The full-source rank is validation evidence, not a proposed cheap runtime path. All stage times are local diagnostics with the checked Sage runtime identity; a candidate ID and target online time remain null.
