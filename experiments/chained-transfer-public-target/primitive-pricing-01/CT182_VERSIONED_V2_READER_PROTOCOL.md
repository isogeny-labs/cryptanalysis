## Versioned contract frozen before implementation
- v2 manifest schema: \`ct182-counterbalanced-cold-public-process-panel/v2\`; v2 report schema: \`ct182-counterbalanced-cold-transfer-adjudication/v2\`. Manifest MUST include \`order_schedule=ct181-block-packet-parity-ABBA-BAAB/v2\` and a SHA-256 of the ordered full \`(block,index,slot,arm)\` roster. Reader recomputes it, rejecting a mismatch.
- Exact packet shuffle remains \`Random(20261117 + block).shuffle(range(48))\`. Treatment slots from CT181 \`order(block,index)\`: even parity ABBA, odd parity BAAB, followed by two A/A controls. A = \`direct_composite\`; B = \`strongest_retained_lift_chain\`. Any changed order, arm, slot, argv, cohort, counter, child native clock nested total, raw stdout/stderr digest, timing, RSS mismatch or terminal is a refusal.
- Statistics: within each packet-block compare sums of the *actual two A positions* with sums of the *actual two B positions*; log ratio per block. Median eight block log ratios per packet. Group by the frozen 16 direction/sector/identity strata; median packet logs per stratum and packet-cluster bootstrap for strata with more than one unique packet. Do not pool the sole identity packet into a CI. Report A/A drift and separate ABBA/BAAB diagnostics. Position-only stationary null must give exactly 1.0; a separable true ratio 1.25 must yield 1.25.
- The reader is offline: output claims \`complete_single_target_ecdlp_advantage_established=false\`, \`signed_independent_pcc=false\`, \`original_full_695_701_custody_replayed=false\`. Never assign a signed verdict or infer paid/native execution.
- Tests must cover null, programmed true effect, full synthetic 2,304-line roster, corrupt order, wrong v1 schema and incomplete/censored records; run normal and \`python -O\` (no \`assert\` for validation). Source CI is *not* native parity, transfer pricing or complete cold solve.

Adopt the same distinct v2 schema/roster in a future reconciled collector before running *any* real panel.
