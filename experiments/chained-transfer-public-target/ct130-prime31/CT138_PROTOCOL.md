Date: 2026-11-15. Standing: [pcc:unverified]. Separate child of cryptanalysis PR #1547 (CT136) and crypto root PR #3877. Frozen **before creating the CT138 executable or executing new seeds**. This is a portable toy CM-neighbor comparison, NOT an original-prime Jacobian-chain or signed complete attack.

## Hypothesis and competing outcomes

CT136's 32-lane Montgomery batch-inversion implementation produced a 1.238737x scope-limited hosted ratio. Increasing the matched source-lane width to 64 might erase the apparent mapped-target advantage; it could also slow both implementations through larger collision-state/working-set overhead. Width 16 probes the opposite side of the crossover. Test the **same exact public target cohort** with both source and target implementations at each width. A speedup is *not* assumed; every nonwin and failed solve is retained. No opportunity to choose a different seed/curve after results.

Scientific prior art: existing CT135/CT136 protocol, sources, verifier, negative controls, and reports; Gallant-Lambert-Vanstone symmetry is *already* accounted for; Montgomery batch inversion and negation-map rho (Bernstein et al., *On the correct use of the negation map*, https://eprint.iacr.org/2011/003.pdf) warn that arithmetic batching and fruitless cycles affect realized rho speed. Shared research-KB MCP/Conductor unavailable in this runtime; do not make novelty claims. No change to source/target group order or security asymptotics.

## Independent acceptance / strongest comparator

For every row require original-source point and subgroup verification in a separate Python elliptic-curve implementation, exact public scalar reconstructed from independent MT64, full group/isogeny preflight, row ordering, arm names, schema, per-solver public answer, zero missing failures, identical duplicate cold counters, every aggregate, and eleven whole-input negative controls under both `python3 -S` and `python3 -O -S`. The CT138 audit shall reject source+target swaps, missing widths/seeds, corrupted timing or scalar, and forged batch-width labels. Record counts and retain independent per-width positive and mutation results.

Primary comparison is **minimum total time across all source solvers and all tested widths divided by minimum total time across all mapped-target solvers and all tested widths**, on the identical cohort and host. No combining per-target oracle winners. Also report each width's best source/target ratio, A/A, per-target paired bootstrap as descriptive not a population CI, and peak RSS. A result `>1` only establishes a portable bounded toy comparison against the named implementations, not the owner-approved full-chain claim; `<=1` rejects that scoped advantage.
