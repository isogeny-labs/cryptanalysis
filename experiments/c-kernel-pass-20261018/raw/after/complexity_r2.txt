libcryptanalysis 0.1.0 benchmark: complexity

Empirical time complexity.  The measured group-operation cost of each
algorithm is fitted to C * N^alpha, so the exponent (the O()) is measured
rather than assumed and the constant is comparable across algorithms.
scope: global = whole algorithm, step = one phase of a method.

Z_p^*:  cost ~ C * N^alpha, fitted over 5 sizes (N = group order).
| algorithm       | scope  | theory   | fitted alpha | R^2    | const @ N^theory |
|-----------------|--------|----------|--------------|--------|------------------|
| bsgs            | global | N^0.500 |        0.487 | 0.9993 |            1.508 |
| rho             | global | N^0.500 |        0.477 | 0.9903 |            1.781 |
| kangaroo        | global | N^0.500 |        0.526 | 0.9910 |            1.843 |
| grumpy          | global | N^0.500 |        0.502 | 0.9966 |            1.264 |
| precomp build   | step   | N^0.667 |        0.647 | 0.9954 |            1.192 |
| precomp online  | step   | N^0.333 |        0.347 | 0.8768 |            2.781 |
