# Smooth-coordinate selection in the M31 auxiliary curve

Among all positive 47-smooth integer representatives below 2³¹−1, 249,155 meet the auxiliary full-order coordinate criterion and 124,093 lift on the target. The eligible seeds identify 247,386 distinct 32-coordinate fibers. They contain 3,957,125 target lifts, averaging 15.995752 per fiber against the unrestricted mean of 15.998747. Of these fibers, 26,416 contain at least 20 lifts. Smoothness of the positive auxiliary x-coordinate at this bound selects 1.475% of all fibers and gives no mean-yield advantage on this fixture.

For each prime q in ascending order, census.gp expands the list by multiplying every earlier smooth value by positive powers of q through p−1. Unique prime factorization gives one occurrence per positive smooth integer. The script tests whether x and x²+1 are nonsquares. For every representative through the 13-smooth bound it also checks this criterion directly by testing rational auxiliary lifts and multiplying by 2³⁰ on y²=x³+x. The full-order coset modulo the 32-point subgroup is identified up to sign by the x-coordinate of [32]P. Each distinct fiber is traversed once, using rational point additions and direct target lift checks on y²=x³+2x+14.

Run `gp -q -f census.gp </dev/null` to reproduce results.txt. Each row gives the prime bound, smooth integers, eligible seeds, lifted seeds, distinct fibers, total lifts on these fibers, and fibers with at least 20 lifts. The unrestricted population totals are from the sibling m31-lift-census field scan. Run Lean 4.28 with Mathlib on Smooth.lean to check the exact mean difference given the counts and the universal exclusion of positive powers of two by the nonsquare criterion. The enumeration measures this specific coordinate choice and imposes no smoothness condition on other quantities.
