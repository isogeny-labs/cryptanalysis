# Target-order torsion on the M31 quadratic twist

Let p = 2147483647 and N = 2147505293. The curve with coefficients (2,14) has order N over the base field. Its quadratic twist (2,−14) has order 2147462003, giving twist trace 21645. These are the orders in Card.lean. Over the quadratic extension an isomorphism from the twist to the original curve sends (x,y) to (−x,iy), where i² = −1. Frobenius sends i to −i. Under this identification it acts on the twist by the negative of the original Frobenius.

Since N is prime and differs from p, the geometric N-torsion has dimension two over the field of N elements. Frobenius has characteristic polynomial X² − 21645X + p on the twist. Modulo N this factors as (X+1)(X+p) with distinct roots, so it has an eigenbasis. A point is rational over the extension of degree d precisely when the d-th power fixes it. The elliptic-curve inputs are the standard action on prime-to-characteristic torsion and the Frobenius fixed-point criterion. [Torsion representation, Sutherland lecture 22](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.786/2024/LectureNotes22.pdf) · [Fixed points, lecture 8](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.783/2019/LectureNotes8.pdf)

TwistExtension.lean checks the factorization, distinctness, multiplicative orders and the fixed-coordinate criterion for all degrees. The two orders are 2 and 536876323 = 811 × 661993. At positive degree d the dimension is the sum of the indicators for divisibility by these two orders. The quadratic extension therefore gives one direction, identified with the original rational N-torsion. The first odd degree with any twist N-torsion is 536876323. The first degree with both directions is 1073752646. This excludes an extra independent target-order direction from the twist at small extension degrees. It leaves auxiliary-coordinate filtering and nonhomomorphic correspondences open. PCC-20261123-3aa69f binds the modular theorems. [pcc:unverified]
