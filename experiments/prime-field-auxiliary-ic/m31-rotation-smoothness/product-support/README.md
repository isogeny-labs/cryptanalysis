# Integer products supporting reciprocal rotation pairs

For each bound 47, 67, 71, 73, 79 and 83, enumerate all odd smooth positive representatives below the M31 prime. Retain nonsquares. Group them by their 31st power and join distinct reciprocal groups, keeping every representative cross-product. Publish the sorted integer products and their individual pair counts. Count a pair once in the union even when several products support it.

Replay using just the product list. Factor each integer, enumerate divisors d with both d and N/d below p, and classify their field elements by minimum bit rotation. Require equality of complete unordered pair sets for each product with the exhaustive join. A list alone certifies the pairs it generates. Completeness at a particular bound still depends on exhaustive discovery. Compare the largest product's share and the number of multiply supported pairs across bounds.
