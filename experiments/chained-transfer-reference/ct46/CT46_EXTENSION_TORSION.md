## Precise advance beyond CT45

CT45's relation `4X=0` applies to maps `E[m] -> F[m]` that commute with **Frobenius over F_101**. It must not be extrapolated to every finite extension. Over `F_(101^k)`, the relevant action is the **k-th power** of Frobenius.

For the two fixed ordinary elliptic factors, with traces `-7,-11` and p=101, use the companion matrices

```
C_t = [[0,-101],[1,t]].
```

## Proof mechanism

Put `u_t(0)=2`, `u_t(1)=t`, `u_t(2j)=u_t(j)^2-2*101^j`, and `D_j=u_-7(j)-u_-11(j)`. Then

```
D_(2j)=D_j (u_-7(j)+u_-11(j)).
```

For j a power of two, the trace sum is always 2 modulo 4 (initially -18, and after each doubling each individual trace is 3 modulo 4). Since `D_1=4`, the elementary induction proves

```
v_2(D_(2^r))=r+2.
```

At torsion modulus `2^s`, choosing `k=2^(s-2)` makes the two characteristic polynomials of `C_E^k` and `C_F^k` identical: traces agree modulo `2^s`, determinants equal `101^k`. Because k is a power of two, the reductions modulo two remain irreducible of degree two, so the matrices are cyclic and therefore conjugate modulo `2^s`. The determinant can be adjusted using the norm map of their unramified quadratic centralizer; its unit norm is surjective. Explicit determinant -1 matrices above check this finite claim through modulus 64. Actual Weil-pairing orientations remain an integration obligation.

**Geometric non-isogeny is unaffected.** These finite étale Galois-module isomorphisms do not extend to nonzero elliptic-curve morphisms; CT37's distinct geometric CM fields still force `Hom(E,F)=0` over the algebraic closure. A finite-torsion gluing candidate does not yield a cheap DLP solution.
