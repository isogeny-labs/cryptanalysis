# Fourteen-position prime-norm schedule and fixed-radix measurement

This report records two distinct findings for the secp256k1 fixed-base
`τ`-radix evaluator: an exact integer search for a lower direct point payload,
and a Tier 1 native throughput comparison for the fixed `371−τ` schedule.
The latter does not measure the ordered schedule selected by the screen.

## Search and correctness evidence

The screen enumerates all 50 prime norms `T²−3T+3` for `T=300…600`, checks
residue/sign/unit covariance and exact radius contractions, then runs four
declared coordinate-descent starts (`371`, `380`, `394`, `400`) with choices
from `T=330…430`. The selected local schedule is
`394−τ, 380−τ, 380−τ, (371−τ)×11`. It lowers the computed direct point
payload from 22,313,920 to 22,267,136 bytes, a 46,784-byte reduction. The
coordinate search is local; no global optimum over ordered schedules is
claimed. Re-running `screen14.py` reproduced `screen14.json` byte-for-byte.

`replay14.py` reverses the quotient steps and checks the terminal table and
point action against independent binary secp256k1 multiplication on 164
scalars. Its regenerated output matched `replay14.json` byte-for-byte. The
conditional terminal-radius theorem and its axioms audit are recorded in
`OrderedPrimeNorm14Bound.lean`, `lean-check.log`, and
`lean-fresh-check.log`; the selected-digit proof and concrete group-action
bridge remain explicitly open.

## Native measurement

The frozen panel compares fixed `371−τ` at 14 positions with the fixed
15-position `268−τ` reference on 12,288 same-point outputs, with 16
alternating repetitions, 48 paired comparisons, and adjacent A/A controls.
The committed independent audit replayed every arm and gate; regenerated
`analyze_fixed14_tier1.py` output matched the committed analysis byte-for-byte.

| Measure | Fixed 14 (`371−τ`) | Reference 15 (`268−τ`) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Mean online time per 12,288 outputs | 135.822 ms | 138.354 ms |
| Mean table preparation | 3,953.993 ms | 2,103.098 ms |
| Retained native table | 26,035,988 bytes | 12,336,756 bytes |

Reference/candidate online time was 1.020033×, with the committed repeat-
bootstrap 95% interval [1.016715, 1.023710]. The interval is an
instance-scoped Tier 1 result; the RunPod CPU affinity does not establish
physical-host isolation. Preparation and retained-memory costs are higher
for the measured fixed-14 evaluator. The ordered schedule's native cost and
throughput remain unmeasured.
