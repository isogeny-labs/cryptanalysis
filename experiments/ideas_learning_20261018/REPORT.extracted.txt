AI research execution controls, 18 October 2026
These are actual bounded, CPU-only controls for audit items 8, 10, 11 and 12. They produced no new index-calculus speedup, safe learned rejection rule, or resolution of an open problem.
The useful outcomes are a negative branching comparison, detection of empty-base training failure, an exact finite bucket signal, and a degree surrogate whose calibration limits are explicit.
All calculations used one worker per script with no GPU and F4_F2_ECHELON=host. Container timings are unqualified exploratory CPU diagnostics. They are not host-isolated performance
evidence. No complete IC pipeline, same-target rho comparison, or deployed-curve attack ran here.

Item 11b: the actual single-bucket question
bucket_gain.py separately measured the statistic requested by crypto-autoresearcher:knowledge/open-problems/KN-OPEN-91d4f6.md: G=M_eff*max_(s,c) Pr[h(Q+R)=c | h(Q) XOR
h(R)=s]. The offset is s XOR c. This is a maximum over conditional buckets, not average agreement.
The exact subgroups have orders 29 at n7 and 2,003 at n13. The maps are F2-linear x-functionals with j=1..4, alongside an x-only SHA-derived null matched to the effective image and the
same ambient XOR alphabet. Nonempty images need not have power-of-two size on a finite subgroup; the first failed assumption and its correction are retained in
BUCKET-AMENDMENT-01.json.
At n13 the strongest of the tested linear maps has G=1.4375, while its matched null has G=1.0817. That is a measured finite level-one signal. Two tiny subgroups do not establish growing
gain or an asymptotic attack. At n7 finite-image effects are large and the null can exceed the linear map.
The halving trace is constant on both odd-order subgroups and has effective gain 1. Exact full-group controls give trace gain 2 and a point-based quotient-of-order-four instrument gain 4.
The latter uses the full toy group's cyclic table and is not proposed as an efficient x-only filter. All pair totals and FFT rounding residuals were checked; n7 results were independently
checked by direct pair enumeration. Exact numerator and denominator tables are in bucket-results.json. This measurement leaves the open problem open.

Item 10: restricted program search returns the incumbent
The first restricted scalar-coordinate search preserved 17 candidate/control records but encountered empty-base degeneracy. That is retained in program-results.json. It cannot show useful
progress.

A separately frozen experiment, PROGRAM-02-PROTOCOL.json, searches actual group-law query kernels. Targets are supplied only as points; scalar labels are confined to an
independent correctness oracle. The grammar contains ordered exhaustive triples, a pair-sum hash table, and sorted pair sums, combined with five scalar-multiplied subspace predicates.
Both signs are explicitly cofactor-projected, identities discarded, and duplicates removed. Projection/setup/query operations and every candidate's search cost are charged.
After an explicitly retained empty-base admission at ell3, the amended ell4 training run at n7,b1 selected multiplier1 with the existing pair-hash kernel from 15 candidates, matching the
baseline's six usable points. Its fitness was 288 counted group additions/probes/comparisons, including projection. The heldout curve n13,b7 was constructed only after selection. It has
subgroup order 53 and 16 projected usable points. Both the selected program and incumbent took 820 counted units, decomposing all 52 nonidentity targets. Thus the search found no new
improvement.
All target answers agreed with exhaustive triple-sum membership and positive witness replay. Across search and holdout the charged candidate work was 39,587 units; the run used
2.149112 CPU seconds. Multiplicity totals equal B^3 for every base. Different base supports are reported separately and do not constitute a free yield improvement. See
program-group-results.json and program-group-inputs.json.
This is restricted enumerative program search, not an AlphaEvolve/LLM loop. Complete IC cost, calibrated unit sensitivities, compiled kernel mutation, strong rho and independent pipeline
holdouts remain owed.

Item 12: fit the real archive, respect calibration units
surrogate.py admitted 1,718 unique pdp-degree-profile/1 aggregate-cell receipts from nine exact Koblitz curves (n=13,19,23,29,31,37,41,43,47). It retained source-file hashes, line numbers,
workload/base/source identities and all four censored/incomplete cells. It excluded 36 collection-run rows with a different semantic schema. There were no duplicate or conflicting run IDs in
this source set.
The response is completed-cell maximum recorded MutantXL D_solve, not Boolean F4 solving degree. Counted cost is a separate response. Censored observations retain their observed
lower bounds and are excluded from exact response fitting; they remain in a completion classifier. That classifier's heldout accuracy merely ties the majority baseline and falsely predicts two
incomplete cells complete. Its apparent 99.47% accuracy is unhelpful.
Shape-group holdout used 1,017 training, 322 complete calibration and 375 complete test cells. The fixed random forest's degree MAE was 0.07048 and descriptive 90% split-interval
coverage was 89.6%; the median baseline's MAE was 1.376. Counted-cost log2 MAE was 0.39583. These are observations on a selected, heterogeneous historical archive, not a coverage
theorem.
The stricter surrogate_curve.py keeps whole exact curves out of training. Its 383 test cells on n23/n31 give degree MAE 0.10041 and counted-cost log2 MAE 0.63061. Only three
independent calibration curves exist in that split. A 90% group-conformal quantile requests rank four, so the correct interval is unbounded. It cannot price an expensive future run with a
finite calibrated upper bound. This limitation is in surrogate-curve-results.json.
Per-cell matrix memory is not fitted from cumulative main-process peak RSS. Unseen chained presentations and the proposed n23/ell6 or n29/ell7 m4 cells are rejected as unsupported. The
archive has fixed orders and is not a complete presentation/order surface. Individual historical target receipts and executable historical snapshots were not reconstructed, so the full
receipt-promotion gate remains open. Further independent curves, actual F4 degree/memory receipts and prospective validation are required.
