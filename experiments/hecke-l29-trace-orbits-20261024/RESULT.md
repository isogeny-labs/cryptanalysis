For each squarefree fiber, a 30-by-30 moment system determines a polynomial `y = h(x)`. Factoring the `x`-polynomial then gives one point over each factor field; summing its Frobenius orbit yields a rational trace point. Across **54 frozen source points**, these short trace relations raise the induced rank on the original 3,949 orbit columns from **3,947 to 3,948**. The sign-augmented anchored kernel falls from dimension **2 to 1**, and one independently recovered generic seed log completes the expanded orbit table.

The other two already have fully rational rows in the previous result. The additional 20 sources are the five torsion translates of each of the four residual ambiguous old orbit representatives. Their exact points and factor-degree profiles are frozen in the [factor records](factor_result.json) and [residual factor records](residual_factor_result.json). All 54 squarefree fibers were reconstructed and independently replayed, including factors of degrees 2, 3, 5, 7, 14, 15, and 29.
