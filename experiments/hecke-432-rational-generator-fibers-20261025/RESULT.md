# Restricted fiber census and a new extension-generator image at p=883

The checked forward map from cyclic order-432 level structures to the optimal curve \(B=432a1:y^2=x^3-16\) has a sharply measured input boundary at \(p=883\). Exhausting all \(\mathbf F_{883}\)-isomorphism classes and all subgroups generated by rational points of exact order 432 gives 54 level structures. They map to 12 distinct points of the 931-point group \(B(\mathbf F_{883})\), with six fibers of size three and six of size six. Those 12 points all have order 133 and together generate the full group of order 931. A further Frobenius-stable cyclic subgroup with generator first rational over \(\mathbf F_{883^3}\) maps to \((216,882)\in B(\mathbf F_{883})\), outside that 12-point image. This is an explicit increase in forward-map coverage from admitting an extension-field generator.

## Exhaustive rational-generator domain

Hasse's interval for \(\#E(\mathbf F_{883})\) is \([825,943]\). A rational point of order 432 requires the group order to be a multiple of 432, so the only possible order is 864, equivalently trace \(t=20\). `enumerate_classes_sage.py` scans every \(j\in\mathbf F_{883}\): one model and its nonsquare quadratic twist for each generic \(j\), six sextic twists at \(j=0\), and two quartic twists at \(j=1728\). It found 48 isomorphism classes of order 864; 36 have a rational order-432 subgroup, and exhaustive point enumeration and subgroup deduplication found 54 distinct pairs \((E,C_{432})\) in this rational-generator domain.

The [quarter-shift moduli evaluator](../hecke-432-moduli-shift-evaluator-20261025/RESULT.md) and [level-27 coordinate bridge](../hecke-27-coordinate-bridge-20261025/RESULT.md) were applied to each pair, followed by the quadratic twist and unique rational dual-isogeny lift. All 54 passed exact subgroup-order, isogeny, \(j\)-pair inversion, Frobenius-conjugacy, and point-replay checks. `forward_census.json` records every input and image. `audit_coverage_sage.py` independently reconstructs the 54 input subgroups and target points, checks the fiber counts, and computes their additive span.

The 12/931 figure is the exact image of the **rational-generator domain defined above**. A rational cyclic subgroup may also have a generator in an extension field; the next experiment below evaluates one such point. The additive span of the 12 image points is a group calculation, distinct from finding relations or recovering logs for those points.

## Frobenius screen and extension witnesses

For a Frobenius-stable cyclic subgroup \(\langle P\rangle\) of order 432, Frobenius acts as \(F(P)=aP\) for a unit \(a\bmod432\). If \(t\) is the curve trace, the characteristic polynomial \(F^2-tF+883\) annihilates \(P\), so

\[
a^2-ta+883\equiv0\pmod{432}.
\]

The exhaustive necessary-congruence screen over the Hasse trace interval permits \(t\in\{-52,-20,20,52\}\). It is a filter, not an existence assertion for every listed eigenvalue. The two Lean theorems in `formal/TraceBound.lean` check the numeric implications \(a=1\Rightarrow t=20\) and \(a=-1\Rightarrow t=-20\) under this interval; the Frobenius characteristic-polynomial bridge is stated and proved in the paragraph above but remains outside that Lean module.

The first \(t=20\) class in the frozen scan has \(j=6\) and model \(y^2=x^3+526x+109\). The quadratic-extension search found a generator \(P=(657u+646,254u+225)\in E(\mathbf F_{883^2})\) of order 432 with \(F(P)=217P\), where 217 has order two modulo 432. The subgroup is defined over \(\mathbf F_{883}\), while its generator is not. Its forward image is \((281,1)\), already in the rational-generator image.

The cubic-extension search found \(P=(592u^2+588u+734,873u^2+229u+805)\in E(\mathbf F_{883^3})\), also of order 432, with \(F(P)=145P\). The scalar 145 has order three modulo 432. Embedding the generator into \(\mathbf F_{883^6}\) supplies both the cubic generator and the fourth root needed to orient the quarter shifts. The two level-27 outputs are Frobenius conjugates; the resulting twist point is \((130,407)\) on \(y^2=x^3+432\), and its unique rational lift is \((216,882)\) on \(B\). All three embeddings \(\mathbf F_{883^3}\hookrightarrow\mathbf F_{883^6}\) independently returned that same \(B\)-point. `extension3_evaluation_0.json` through `_2.json` retain the exact intermediate values.
