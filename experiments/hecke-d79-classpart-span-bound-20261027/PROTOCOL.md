# Class-part span bound for degree-13 Hecke rows

For each of the ten complete 40-source cases in the three sealed D79 holdouts, take its baseline relation rows B over the recorded largest-prime field F_q, its 40 lifted source class-part vectors C, and its verified degree-13 Hecke rows H. Every H row is a signed sum of one to four C rows. Therefore `rowspan(B ∪ H) ⊆ rowspan(B ∪ C)`, and the achievable Hecke rank gain is at most `rank(B ∪ C) − rank(B)`. The bound uses source-lift output but requires no signed cancellation enumeration. It is a post-lift, pre-enumeration screen, not a pre-lift skip rule.

Freeze the three selection/challenge/audit triples by SHA-256. Recompute B and C ranks independently with Sage for every case and compare the upper bound with audited full Hecke rank gain. For each ascending source prefix, calculate the class-part span bound and the attained Hecke rank from the frozen challenge prefix data where available; retain the first count at which the bound could reach rank 39 and the first count at which actual verified rows reach rank 39. Preserve cases with upper bound below 39 after all 40 sources and any mismatch. Do not infer a solver or online cost from these ranks.

Formalize the general row-span inclusion and rank-gain inequality in Lean 4 over an arbitrary field, finite source/row indices, arbitrary baseline vectors, and derived rows as finite coefficient sums of source vectors. Audit theorem axioms and pin the Lean/mathlib revisions. The bridge between stored integer source coefficients, the curve equations, and this abstract field-vector model is checked separately by Sage and documented in the proof map.
