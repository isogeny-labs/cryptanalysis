# Source-fiber denominator exception below ten million

The exact √2 reduction gate certifies absence of rational C–E pairs at **83,036 of 83,047** primes `7 ≤ p < 10,000,000` with `p ≡ 11 (mod 24)`. The eleven exceptions have now been directly classified: six primes have rational C–E pairs, and the frozen inverse B branch gives four valid C–E–B triples, all at `p=131`. The new `1,000,000 ≤ p < 10,000,000` interval contains 73,238 prime cells, 73,237 of which pass the exact gate. Its sole exception is `p=1,237,499`; direct source-fiber and resolvent calculations give two rational C points and zero rational E roots.

The new exceptional prime divides the source-y denominator norm and the rational coefficient denominator LCM. It does **not** divide either conjugate-pair resultant norm. The denominator failure occurs at the third rational root `x_C=1,042,948` of the degree-18 field polynomial. There the source-y numerator and denominator both vanish, but the curve discriminant `4x_C³−27` has quadratic-character value `−1` modulo `1,237,499`, so this x-coordinate has no rational y-coordinate on `C: y²+y=x³−7`. The two rational source points, `(906005,729126)` and `(994891,1234531)`, have nonzero source-y denominators and no rational roots of the specialized C–E resolvent. This identifies why the global norm gate was conservative at this prime.

## Frozen test and independent checks

[`protocol.json`](protocol.json) fixed the 10-million exclusive cap, hashes of the exact certificates, and a prospective prediction: every rational C–E pair at a **new octic-pair collision prime** would have zero inverse B-branch denominator. [`scan.py`](scan.py) found no new octic collision prime in this interval; the branch prediction was therefore **not tested** by this holdout. It found only the source-coordinate denominator exception above. The result is in [`scan_result.json`](scan_result.json).

[`exception_protocol.json`](exception_protocol.json) froze that new prime and the original source-fiber method before direct classification. [`classify_exception_sage.py`](classify_exception_sage.py) reconstructed the original source-j equation on `C` modulo `p`, checked that its y-resultant is a nonzero scalar times the square of the frozen degree-18 field polynomial, enumerated all rational source points, and solved the specialized degree-12 resolvent. [`exception_result.json`](exception_result.json) records the two C points and zero C–E pairs. The Sage job used the repository's checked launcher; [`sage-runtime-info.txt`](sage-runtime-info.txt) records the accepted build.

[`audit_exception.py`](audit_exception.py) independently reconstructed the y-resultant from integer polynomial arithmetic, factored the degree-18 polynomial with SymPy, checked the curve discriminant at each of its three rational x-roots, and recomputed the source and E root lists. Its [`exception_audit.json`](exception_audit.json) matches the Sage result. [`audit_scan.py`](audit_scan.py) independently sieved all primes below the cap, repeated every norm and denominator divisibility test, and reconciled the exceptional cell; [`scan_audit.json`](scan_audit.json) has status `PASS_INDEPENDENT_TEN_MILLION_SIEVE_AND_EXCEPTION_REPLAY`.

## Proof map and boundary

The exact conjugate-factor and Frobenius bridge is inherited from the [resultant gate](../hecke-432-j1728-sqrt2-resultants-20261026/RESULT.md). For the new exception, [`SourceDiscriminant.lean`](formal/SourceDiscriminant.lean) proves over an arbitrary field that an x-coordinate with nonsquare `4x³−27` cannot lift to a point of `C`; the checked [`lean-check.txt`](formal/lean-check.txt) reports only `propext` and `Quot.sound` under `#print axioms`. The modular nonresidue calculation, degree-36 resultant identity, and finite-field root lists are independently executable certificates; their large concrete arrays are not yet encoded in Lean. The toolchain and mathlib revision are pinned in the Lean receipt.

The cap is finite. The norm numerators retain unfactored residuals, so the next proof step is to identify their remaining prime divisors or derive a local residue criterion that bypasses global norms when their zeros lie away from rational source points.
