# Inert degree-2 geometry restores eight independent conductor-2 rows

**Contribution.** In the prospectively selected $D=-35$, $p=11131$ class, 2 is inert in the maximal CM order. Each conductor-1 rational $X_0(11)$ point has **three distinct downward degree-2 neighbors**, rather than the unique descendant seen in the split-2 classes. The 12 conductor-2 degree-3 rows therefore add **eight** to the full rational relation rank after the other 16 rows. Four exact commutator relations account for the remaining four rows. The full 28-row matrix has rank **24** over the rationals; its nonfloor projection has rank **20**. This confirms the frozen positive-increment prediction and identifies the local degree-2 branching rule as the cause.

For a conductor-1 point $P$, write $T_2P=Q_1+Q_2+Q_3$, where all three $Q_i$ have conductor 2, and $T_3P=W_1+W_2+W_3+W_4$. With $R_2=T_2+2I$ and $R_3=T_3+I$, Hecke commutation gives

```
R3(Q1) + R3(Q2) + R3(Q3)
    = R2(W1) + R2(W2) + R2(W3) + R2(W4) + R2(P) - 2 R3(P).
```
