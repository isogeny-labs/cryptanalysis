# A frozen nonperiodic class reveals the missing X₀(11) eligibility gate

The preregistered selector chose `D = −551`, conductor 6 and `p = 12703`, with norm-2 and norm-3 class orders both 26. Its conditional row-space prediction was four floor directions from norm-2 cycles and zero from the non-period-three norm-3 cycles. The independent fiber gate instead found **zero rational X₀(11) points above all 156 rational CM j-invariants**. The class walk and matrix were therefore not run, and the floor-rank prediction remains unmeasured at this field.

The obstruction is visible before building any Hecke row. The Frobenius characteristic-polynomial discriminant is `176² − 4·12703 = −551·6²`, which is `8 mod 11`, a nonsquare. Its characteristic polynomial has no root over `F₁₁`. A rational cyclic subgroup of order 11 would be a Frobenius-stable line in the 11-torsion and would give such an eigenvalue. Thus the selected isogeny class has no rational 11-isogeny, matching every saved fiber result. This explains the failure through the modular-curve input condition rather than the later norm-3 dependency mechanism.

The gate returned 26, 26, 52 and 52 rational j-invariants at conductors 1, 2, 3 and 6, and zero rational X₀(11) points in every stratum. `validate_gate.py` independently replays the frozen hashes, prime/discriminant arithmetic and nonresidue check. `candidate_id` and `timing_claim` remain null. The next selector must impose the Frobenius discriminant's quadratic-residue condition modulo 11 before choosing a class for this X₀(11) producer.
