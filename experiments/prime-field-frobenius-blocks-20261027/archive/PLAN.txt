Three construction experiments, started 2026-10-27
=================================================

Objective: cheaply construct points with labels T=uP+vQ while deliberately
placing usable coordinates in a domain that also makes relation finding cheap.
Constant-degree auxiliary lifts alone do not meet the full objective.

1. Quadratic fixed-norm lifts.
   Keep the actual sampled base and Tr_E(sum R_i)=O. Verify the reciprocal
   sextic divisor identity over the extension, with coefficients over F_p.
   Compress the sextic to a cubic in Z+nu/Z. Measure construction rejections,
   chosen-base polynomial degrees, and a trace-aware line-fiber solver against
   batched direct pairs on exactly the same accepted trace points.

2. Cubic fixed-two-coefficient lifts.
   Derive and implement the quartic projection for q0=-c, q1=d. Validate all
   solutions exhaustively on small fields. Count irreducibility failures and
   common-field embeddings. Independently verify Frobenius traces and the
   degree-nine divisor/composition identity for actual chosen-base relations.
   Use the same relation-finding control as in experiment 1.

3. Arithmetic-progression blocks.
   Implement the exact symmetric-block S_3 resultant certificate using baby
   and giant points. Count certificate construction, failed blocks, poles,
   extraction and complete index recovery. Compare to sequential scanning and
   interval multi-target BSGS. An O~(B+sqrt(M)) algorithm is a target, not a
   claimed bound. Ordinary quotient-ring resultants cost at least the measured
   work of their implementation; no free modular-resultant oracle is assumed.

All timed comparisons run serially and share inputs. Failed systems count.
Full-log tables are allowed only in independent audits, never in constructions.
Reports distinguish checked identities, measured costs, conjectures, and gaps.
Publication: prepare a research note and self-contained reproduction scripts;
do not promote an unsupported sub-square-root or complete attack claim.
