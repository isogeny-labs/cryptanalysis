The gate `base-plus-direct rank ≥ 37` was frozen in the prior p90917 holdout. Keep that threshold unchanged. This series selects four subsequent D79 conductor-four classes without degree-13 outputs, records each gate decision, then challenges all four with full source pools. It is a bounded replication, not a final estimate of a rare false-reject rate.

Scan odd `v=303,305,...,501`, taking `p=v²+316`. Eligible p must be prime, satisfy `5 | #E(F_p)` and `5 ∤ p−1`, and have largest prime factor of `#E(F_p)` at least 53 for `E: y²+y=x³−x²−10x−20`. Inspect at most the first 16 eligible primes and select the first four whose D79 conductor-four class has exactly 40 x-then-y sorted points. Retain every ineligible-free checked class, size mismatch, construction error and selection shortage. Do not replace a selected class after any degree-13 result.

For each selected class, build the degree-2/5 relation rows and enumerate all direct signed group relations with support at most four. Compute rank over its largest prime-order field. Freeze `LIFT` for rank ≥37 and `SKIP` otherwise, with the class, direct rows, exact field and source identities hash-sealed before degree-13 work. Then lift **all 40 sources in ascending point-index order** for each selected class using the pinned degree-13 function cache. Retain every source result, failure and timing. If all 40 are valid, enumerate signed outside-trace cancellations at support at most four, verify derived class rows in the group, compute the full rank and score the frozen decision against rank ≥39. If any source is censored or infrastructure fails, score `UNKNOWN`, not correct.

Independent checked-Sage replay must re-evaluate the public prescreen, selected class points and gate ranks, all 40 source equations and final relation rows for every complete case.
