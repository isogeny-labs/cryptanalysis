# Historical xedni formalization and corrected witness hypothesis

The sealed [original report](RESULT.md), source, dependency record and build
receipt remain historical evidence with their original hashes.

Its lift-obstruction hypothesis bounds **every** nontrivial relation. That
hypothesis already excludes dependence: any nonzero relation can be scaled
beyond a fixed coefficient bound. It cannot serve as the proposed height
bridge for dependent tuples.

The [corrected successor](../xedni-bounded-witness-20261030/RESULT.md) requires
that existence of a nontrivial relation supplies **some** nontrivial bounded
witness relation. It corrects both the single-tuple and all-compatible-lifts
statements. The height-theoretic witness bound and curve-selection sampling
model remain open. Fresh native verification of the successor is pending.
