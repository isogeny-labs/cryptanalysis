# AES distinguisher laboratory: frozen first suite

**Status at freeze: implementation and bounded synthetic protocol.** Actual runs
are recorded under a fresh directory; planned commands are not results. This
study does not execute the held arms of GOAL-AES-001, GOAL-AES-002 or GOAL-AES-003,
change their status, or satisfy their frontier/certificate requirements.

## Question and acceptance criteria

Can an auditable native instrument rediscover simple reduced-round endpoint
signals, validate them on new fixed keys, reject search-selected random-control
candidates, reproduce the known three-round integral, and localize which
algebraic relationships are component-only? A software pass is not a new attack.

Before running, commit the exact sources, lockfile, this protocol and runner.
The runner then binds the executable hash, source hashes and protocol hashes.
Inputs are public synthetic fixtures, generated by the binary's recorded
ChaCha20/SHA-256 domains. Initial whitening, column-major state and last-round
MixColumns conventions are explicit. No deployed system, user key, ciphertext
collection or external attack oracle is used.

## Frozen cells

The runner is the exact command card. Its twelve differential families are:

- AES-128 at 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 and 10 rounds, last MixColumns omitted;
- AES-128 at 3 rounds, last MixColumns retained;
- standard AES-192/12 and AES-256/14;
- random-permutation discovery/validation controls at the configurations marked
  1 and 10 rounds (these are control labels, not AES executions).

Each family uses 16 input differences, 256 discovery pairs per key, 2 discovery
keys, the best 2 frozen events, 4,096 validation pairs per event/key and 8 new
validation keys. Seeds are the exact literals in `run.sh`; they are never changed
in response to an outcome. Validation's per-family alpha is `0.0005`. Across all
12 families, the union-bound allocation is at most `0.006`; each binary report
also corrects for both arms, events and keys. No pooled-only success is admitted.

Other cells are exact DDT/LAT/BCT enumeration; bounded 1–4-round beam proposals
(`beam=8`, `branch=4`, last MixColumns retained); five integral configurations
(128/3 with and without final mixing, 128/4 with mixing, 192/3 and 256/3 with
mixing), 8 keys and 2 structures each; 65,536 component quartets; 8 one-bit
key-schedule fixtures per AES variant; one fixed-delta 1-round boomerang diagnostic
with 4,096 pairs; and 64 examples on 2 keys in each dataset split. The exported
dataset is a plumbing fixture; no model is trained.

## Controls, interpretation and stopping

- **Cipher correctness:** all three FIPS vectors, all supported round/convention
  roundtrips, independent OpenSSL comparisons, and explicit two-byte cancellation.
- **Known positive:** a one-round endpoint signal and a three-round integral.
- **Nulls:** consistent lazy PRPs, independent discovery/validation keys, and
  random-source search followed by independent validation.
- **Nonvacuity:** changing one member of an integral set must alter the observed
  XOR sum in the exercised fixtures. Raw AES output-affine cross-ratios are
  compared with inversion and affine-undone controls, not assumed invariant.
- **Frozen evaluation:** full prescribed samples, no early significance stop,
  event tuning or seed replacement. Every key/event count survives in JSON.
- **Replay:** the first validation is repeated exactly into a new file and
  compared byte-for-byte. Replay checks determinism, not independent replication.
- **Limits:** one local CPU process; `--max-pairs=10000000` admission guard on
  discovery/validation; no cloud compute or GPU. A process failure stops the card
  and preserves its artifacts; unstarted cells remain pending. Fixed samples are
  experimental design, not a cap on the user's broader research program.
- **Accounting:** raw pair/query counts and beam transitions are instrument
  counts. No throughput, best-implementation, end-to-end key-recovery cost,
  security-bit reduction or asymptotic claim is made. Receipt timestamps are
  process provenance, not a controlled speed comparison.

If positives fail or nulls trigger, investigate the instrument and withhold its
dependent candidate claims. A bounded negative at later rounds constrains only
this finite search family and sensitivity. Zero observations never certify an
impossible differential. A later independent replication or expanded family gets
a new protocol/output directory; old records are immutable.

## Execute and inspect

```sh
cargo test --release --locked --manifest-path tools/aes-discover/Cargo.toml
bash experiments/aes-distinguisher-lab-20261017/run.sh \
  tools/aes-discover/target/release/aes-discover \
  experiments/aes-distinguisher-lab-20261017/runs/NEW-RUN-ID
```

The output directory must not already exist. `commands.tsv`, `source-sha256.txt`,
`binary-sha256.txt`, `environment.txt`, per-command logs, `status.tsv`, raw JSON,
dataset sidecars and replay verdict are the evidence. Reports/graphs/PDF sit next
to the immutable run directory, not inside it. Future official campaign adoption
still requires its ordinary Coordinator authorization and independent review.
