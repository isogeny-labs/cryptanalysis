# CM arithmetic addition to the Lean corpus

The new source adds four checked integer/natural-number theorems. The corpus
now has 96 research sources: the same 91 historical sources and five fresh
sources rebuilt. Earlier 94- and 95-source receipts remain unchanged.

The [proof map](../../experiments/ghs-sweeper/cm-formal/README.md) preserves
the full written geometric statement and lists missing bridges. This is
partial arithmetic formalization, not an evaluated isogeny or full Lean
geometry proof. CI compares the new `receipt.json` against a fresh build.

Three mutable gate files and the active problem record have newer versions.
The prior 89-artifact record remains valid at commit 9ddbe2c6; its historical
bytes are preserved. The new quartic/CM manifest records the changed paths
and old/new hashes, while the earlier frozen reports and arithmetic examples
remain unchanged. No frozen manifest is regenerated to hide a change.
