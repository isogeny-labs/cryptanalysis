# Blind mathematical brief for reviewer A

Evaluate the following natural-number implication without reading any
producer proof, source, report, or sibling review.

For `p,r,n,m,k,l : Nat`, define `delta(x,c)` to be `x-c` when `c≤x` and
`c-x` otherwise. Assume `n=k*r`, `m=l*r`, `delta(n,p+1)^2≤4p`,
`delta(m,p+1)^2≤4p`, and `16p<r^2`. Does `n=m` follow? Derive or refute it.
Check whether the premises have positive nonvacuous examples, zero cases,
and whether strictness is necessary. In particular, test the weakened
condition `16p≤r²` with `p=1,r=4,n=0,m=4,k=0,l=1`. State what extra facts
would be needed to apply the implication to a concrete elliptic curve order.

Report actual materials read, sibling access, and any protocol deviation.
