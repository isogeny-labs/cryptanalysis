# Blind arithmetic brief for reviewer A

Review the following mathematical statement from its hypotheses alone. Do
not read any source proof, README, receipt, review plan or other reviewer's
work. Write an independent derivation or counterexample, assess whether the
assumptions are satisfiable, and test the strict bound with a nearby false
case.

For natural numbers `r`, `width`, `n`, `m`, `k`, `l`, assume

1. `width < r`;
2. `n = k*r` and `m = l*r`;
3. `n ≤ m+width` and `m ≤ n+width`.

Requested conclusion: `n = m`.

Report whether this is valid, which hypotheses carry the conclusion, and
what it does or does not say about an elliptic-curve order certificate.
List every source read and disclose any access to forbidden producer material.
