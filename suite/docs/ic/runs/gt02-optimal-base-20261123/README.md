Every number is a local measurement, not a claim about a deployed curve.

## Results
1. **char2, m = 2: the gain survives at the baseline's S-optimal dimension on every n = 17..33.** Stage B (fresh curve, d selected on the stage-A curve; here also the argmin on the stage-B curve): paired geomean S_base/S_lead 1.247 (n=17), 1.114 (19), 1.125 (21), 1.123 (23), 1.399 (25), 1.337 (27), 1.136 (29), 1.421 (31), 1.404 (33); every lower 95% bound is >= 1.09 (`B_summary.json`). It does not shrink with n (prediction P5 falsified); it tracks where the discrete dimension grid puts the optimum (one dimension step doubles the columns) and the cofactor, not n.
2. **Each arm at its own best dimension** (best lead S / best baseline S over the dimensions run on the same curve): 1.238, 1.226, 1.446, 1.119, 1.530, 1.330, 1.217, 1.704, 1.397 for n = 17..33. The lead's optimum is one dimension higher than the baseline's at n = 19, 21, 25, 29, 31 (a larger base is affordable once the setup is halved). The gain is real at an optimal base, unlike the Koblitz gain of the registry's oversized divisor.
3. **The mechanism is setup only**: setup GAE falls about 1.9-2.0x (cofactor 2) and up to 3.7x (cofactor 6); trials and relations are identical. Gain at a given dimension grows steeply above the optimum (n=33: 1.01, 1.07, 1.40, 1.85, 1.97 for d = 9..13) and is ~1.00 below it.
4. **m = 3: no gain, the filter is a no-op** (documented m = 2 only): ratio 0.992-1.005 at the baseline's m = 3 optimum, n = 17..29 (stage B, `*_m3_*` rows) and 0.998-1.002 on the seed-A m = 3 ladders. Setup GAE identical.
5. **Prime, recalibrated sizes (23, 30, 47, 58, 96 at m = 2; 31, 50, 79, 119 at m = 3) and registry sizes: ratio 1.000** (identical setup GAE, S identical to 3 decimals; 0.997 at one m = 3 cell, 160 columns), because prime bench curves have cofactor 1 (`prime.md`). Nothing to measure; `ca-ic bench` has no prime cofactor option, so cofactor > 1 prime curves were not tested.
6. **Distance to matched rho** (stage B, negation-walk rho from the baseline arm, 12 runs): baseline at its optimum is 9.2, 11.4, 18.6, 14.7, 30.6, 31.8, 32.8, 69.0, 57.5 x rho for n = 17..33; the lead at its best dimension 7.4, 9.3, 12.9, 13.1, 20.0, 23.9, 26.9, 40.5, 41.1 x rho. Not below rho at any n; the gap widens with n for both arms. Prime baseline at the recalibrated size: 7.1 (16b), 9.2, 14.2, 10.0, 19.0 (24b) x rho.
